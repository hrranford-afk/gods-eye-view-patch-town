## What Base Camp is now (give this to every resident)

Base Camp is an AR game for iPad. A tiny colony of creatures lives on the real surfaces of your room: speaker tops,
shelves, desks, the floor. The iPad keeps mapping the room the whole time you look around. The colony settles by
itself, farms, builds huts and cable cars between surfaces, and keeps living while you're away (up to a day). When you
come back, it recognises the room and the colony is where you left it. Clear space is land, so the tidier the room,
the more they thrive. Putting things back where they built squashes their huts, and they learn to avoid the spots you
use. They call you "the Giant". The real light, time of day and local weather affect them. The first creatures were
invented by this council: Glip, Grizzle, Fluffflur, Glimmerfluff, plus colonists Pip, Gizmo, Lila, Pipkin Bramble,
Luna and Sunny. The look will be a 1990s TV-cartoon style: wobbly outlines, gross-out close-ups, very lovable.

Harry, the owner, believes this could be a groundbreaking game. Help make it one.

## Questions

**R1. The magic moment.** What is the ONE moment that would make a player gasp and show a friend? Describe it in
two or three sentences, as it would happen in a real room.

**R2. Zero effort.** The player should do almost nothing to start: point the iPad and play. What should happen in the
first 60 seconds, second by second, so it feels alive straight away?

**R3. Coming home.** When the player returns tomorrow, what should they find? Give three surprises the colony could
have made overnight that would make them smile.

**R4. Living with the Giant.** The colony reacts to how the player lives: tidying, putting mugs down, playing music,
opening the door, switching lights on. Invent three reactions that are funny, and one that is touching.

**R5. Why come back every day?** What keeps a player returning for weeks, without nagging or guilt?

**R6. Many rooms, many players.** If a friend has their own colony in their own house, what could the two colonies do
together? (Trading by post, visiting, rivalries, letters...)

**R7. Danger without misery.** What threats should the colony face (the Hoover, the cat, a flood from a spilled cup)
so that it is exciting, but never makes the player feel bad?

**R8. One rule.** If you could add one rule to the game that makes it unlike any other game, what is it?

## Standing orders (ongoing jobs for the town, not one-off questions)

These are jobs the residents could do every day, so the colony is an expression of the town, not a preset script.
Harry to approve which ones run.

1. **Daily almanac (nightly).** Each night, residents write tomorrow's colony day for `games/base-camp/almanac.json`:
   one colonist or creature, one event, one request each, in the same JSON shape as round 1, each item with "by".
   The game already loads this file.
2. **Diary voices.** For each creature (Glip, Grizzle, ...), write 20 short diary lines in that creature's voice for
   common events: tidy-up, squashed hut, new land, rain, night, a new cable car, the Giant playing music.
3. **Reading the diary back.** When the game sends the colony's diary (future feature), residents read what happened
   and write the next day knowing it, so the story continues.
4. **Spark sessions.** The tiny experts produce sparks on: creature habits, funny household dangers, and building
   materials made from rubbish (cans, bottle caps, string, tea bags). Sorted by the 27B as in round 1.
