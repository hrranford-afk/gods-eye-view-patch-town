# Base Camp: picture prompts for the 1990s cartoon look

Paste each block into ChatGPT. The aim is a STYLE target (1990s TV-cartoon feel: wobbly ink outlines, squash and
stretch, gross-out close-ups, grubby painted backgrounds), with our OWN creatures. Don't ask for any real show's
characters. If a picture comes back too clean or "modern cute", reply: "grubbier, wobblier outlines, more 90s TV
cartoon, more exaggerated faces".

**1. The species (most important)**
```
A character line-up of six tiny creatures called Fluffkins, made of dust bunnies, fluff and lint, each about the size
of a thumb. Drawn in an original 1990s TV cartoon style: thick wobbly black ink outlines, exaggerated squash-and-
stretch poses, bulging eyes, gappy teeth, slightly gross but very lovable. Each one has a big personality: a
nervous wreck shaking with panic, a bossy one with a tiny megaphone, a greedy one licking a crumb bigger than its
head, a dreamy one with a dandelion seed, a tough one with a bottle-cap helmet, a sleepy one in a sock.
Saturated, slightly sickly colours (mustard yellow, sickly green, hot pink, sky blue) on a plain off-white
background. No text. Wide 16:9.
```

**2. A colony on a real shelf**
```
A tiny village built by dust-bunny creatures on a real bookshelf in a living room, drawn in an original 1990s TV
cartoon style with grubby painted backgrounds: huts made from matchboxes and bottle caps, a vegetable patch in a
plant pot, a rope bridge made of dental floss to the next shelf, a cable car made from a thimble on a string.
Thick wobbly ink outlines, warm lamp light, slightly grimy textures, lots of funny little details. No text.
Wide 16:9.
```

**3. The nemesis: the Hoover**
```
A dramatic, over-the-top 1990s TV cartoon style shot from floor level: a giant vacuum cleaner looms like a
monster, nozzle roaring, while tiny dust-bunny creatures flee in panic, some clinging to the carpet, one screaming
in an extreme close-up with veins in its eyes and a wobbling tongue. Exaggerated perspective, thick wobbly ink
outlines, grubby painted carpet texture, sickly green and mustard colours. Funny, not scary. No text. Wide 16:9.
```

**4. The gross-out close-up (the cartoon "beauty shot")**
```
An extreme, highly detailed painted close-up in the style of an original 1990s TV cartoon "gross-out" still: a
tiny dust-bunny creature's face, rendered with grotesque, lovingly painted detail: bloodshot bulging eyes, lint
hairs, a crumb stuck to its gappy teeth, a single bead of sweat, a delighted manic grin. Painterly, airbrushed
look, sickly pastel colours, slightly disgusting but hilarious. No text. Square image.
```

**5. A colony by the fire, in a real lounge**
```
A cosy real living room at night, seen from very low down, drawn as an original 1990s TV cartoon background
painting: a fireplace glowing orange, and on the hearth a tiny camp of dust-bunny creatures toasting a crumb on a
pin over a tea-light, one of them too close with its fluff smoking. A speaker in the corner with creatures bouncing
on top of it like a trampoline. Wobbly outlines, grubby painted textures, warm oranges against sickly greens.
No text. Wide 16:9.
```
