# Base Camp: waterworks plan (set by CAIRN, 13 Nov 2026)

Harry, 13 Nov: "how we're going to get water and things into the town… getting water, taking the waste to the
toilets, getting fresh water from the sinks." It builds on his M8 idea (council round 4). The town's M8 answers
have not come back yet. When they do, they go in word for word with their "by" credit and replace CAIRN's placeholders.

## What the game has now
- A well or water tower per camp, a water store, and the Water tool (hold to water).
- Nothing comes from the real room yet, and there is no waste.

## Build 18: Waterworks (after the mouse, build 17)
1. **Real water sources.** The AR scan already recognises a sink, toilet, potted plant, cup, bottle and vase.
   Each becomes a water source with its own quality:
   - Sink: fresh water, good for creatures.
   - Plant saucer or vase: green water. Plants love it; creatures burp bubbles.
   - Toilet: the creatures refuse to drink it and hold their noses, but it is fine for the compost.
   - Cup or mug: tea water. Creatures get hyper for a minute.
   Practice room: a toy sink and a plant pot stand in.
2. **Getting it home.**
   - First, a bucket chain: creatures carry thimbles.
   - Then drinking-straw pipes, dragged from a source to a camp like a cable car. Water drips along them.
   - A bottle-cap tank stores it.
3. **Waste, kindly.**
   - A tiny loo hut in each camp.
   - Creatures wheel the waste to a compost heap, which makes crops grow faster.
   - Or they carry it to the real toilet or drain on a "flush run", with a cheer when it is done.
   - Funny, never gross close-ups.
4. **Bad water to good.** A sand-and-charcoal bottle filter cleans green or toilet-side water slowly.
5. **Season goal hook.** Winter adds "keep the pipes from freezing", with a sock lagging the pipes.

## Rules
Bad water is funny, never deadly. Nobody dies. No real brands. The save stays backward-compatible.
The robots test the pacing, so water helps growth and is never a chore.
