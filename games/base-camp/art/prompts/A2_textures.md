# A2: seamless textures for Base Camp (inputs for order art-b1, stage 1)

Use the same prompt for each material, swapping only the MATERIAL words. Make 10 of each.

Prompt:
A seamless tileable texture of MATERIAL, seen flat from straight above, filling the whole square, for a video game.
Grubby, hand-painted, slightly worn, in an original 1990s TV cartoon painting style: wobbly ink lines, saturated
slightly sickly colours. No text, no letters, no logos, no brand marks. 1024x1024.

Materials (8):
1. matchbox cardboard
2. flattened tin can metal, dented
3. knitted sock wool
4. green moss
5. carpet fluff and lint
6. pencil wood with paint chips
7. cork
8. dental floss rope, coiled

Avoid: real brand names, printed words, photo-real looks, clean modern "cute" style.
