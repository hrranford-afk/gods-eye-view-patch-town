# S1: season decorations for Base Camp (inputs for order seasons-1, stage 2)

One picture per object. Each object is shown on its own, centred, against a plain flat light grey background, and the
whole object is in view. Show it in three-quarter view. 1024x1024.
Style: original 1990s TV cartoon. Thick wobbly ink outlines, a grubby painted texture, and slightly sickly, saturated
colours. Everything is made by thumb-sized creatures from household rubbish.
No text, no letters, no logos, no brands. Make 6 of each.

Autumn
1. a pile of fallen leaves made from torn paper scraps and fluff, with a tiny creature-sized dent where someone jumped in
2. a harvest supper table made from a jar lid, with a crumb loaf, a pea and half a grape
3. a paper lantern made from a sweet wrapper on a matchstick pole, glowing warm

Advent and Christmas
4. an advent calendar built from 24 stacked matchboxes, with little numbered doors (the numbers drawn as dots, not digits)
5. a Christmas tree made from a pine cone standing in a bottle cap, decorated with sequins and a foil star
6. a paper chain made from coloured sweet-wrapper strips, hanging in a loop
7. a single knitted stocking, creature-sized, hanging from a thread
8. a snowman made from three balls of white fluff, with a seed nose

Winter to spring
9. a tiny sledge made from a lolly stick and a bent paperclip
10. a flower crown made from daisy petals and thread
