# A6: ceiling things for Base Camp (inputs for order art-b1, stage 2)

Make one picture per object, each a single object on a plain flat light grey background, centred, whole object
in view, three-quarter view from slightly below (the player looks UP at the ceiling). 1024x1024.
Style: original 1990s TV cartoon, thick wobbly ink outlines, grubby painted texture. No text, no logos.
Make 6 of each.

1. a cup hook screwed into a ceiling board, with a tiny pulley wheel and a little bell, a knotted rope hanging down
2. a tiny hot-air balloon made from a used tea bag, with a thimble basket
3. a lookout nest made from an old striped sock, hanging from a string
4. a lantern made from a bottle cap and a paper scrap, hanging on a thread, glowing warm
