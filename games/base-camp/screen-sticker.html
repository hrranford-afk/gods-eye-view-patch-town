Screen sticker

Use a screen that stays in one place (a TV or a desk monitor, not a laptop you carry about) as the room's sticker.

1. Pick a number (one per room):
2. Make it the right size. Hold a bank card flat against the screen over the dashed box. Press − or + until the box is exactly the size of the card.
bank card size

Then: turn off the screen saver and sleep, and turn the brightness up. In the game, lock the room first (find the camp), then look at the screen: the room keeps the sticker. Next time, look at the screen and the camp jumps straight back. Tap or press a key to come back here. The size is remembered on this screen.
