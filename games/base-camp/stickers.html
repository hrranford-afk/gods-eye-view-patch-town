Room stickers for Base Camp

Stick one somewhere flat that stays put, like the side of a bookcase.

  • One sticker for each room. Every sticker has its own pattern, so use a different one in each room.
  • The first time the iPad sees a sticker in a room, that room keeps it. Next time, point the iPad at it and the camp jumps straight back to its place.
  • Cut along the dashed line and keep the white edge around the black square. Do not fold it, and keep shiny tape off the front.

For a grown-up: print at actual size (100%, not "fit to page"). The black square of every sticker must be 8 cm across: the game counts on that. This line should be exactly 10 cm long:

10 cm

On screen the stickers may look smaller or bigger. Only the printed size counts.

Good to know

  • Up on a wall, the side of a cupboard or flat on a shelf all work. Somewhere the iPad can see it from about an arm's length to two metres away is best.
  • If a sticker moves, the colonists notice and remember its new place.
  • If the iPad sees a sticker that belongs to another room, it asks whether you have moved rooms. It never mixes two rooms up by itself.
  • The game only looks at the sticker's pattern. No pictures leave the iPad.

Stickers 1 to 6 are ArUco markers (4 x 4 dictionary, ids 1 to 6), black square 8 cm.
