# Base Camp: developer handover

Branch `game/room-colony`, folder `games/base-camp/`. One file (`index.html`, about 3,000 lines, sectioned by `// =====` banners). three.js r128 from cdnjs. Read README.md first for the player flow. **Systems only: the art is redesigned later (1990s TV-cartoon style), nothing here is final styling.** Never run prettier on `index.html` (long lines are deliberate).

## Build 4: the automatic flow (read this first)
Sections added to `index.html`, in order: **FIELD RECORDER** (`REC`, `WONDERS`, `buildReport`), **AUTOMATIC PLAY** (`AUTO`, `autoTick`, `liveSync`, `autoSettle`, `persist`), **RELOCALISATION** (`RELOC`, `relocJob`, `relocLock`). `G.auto` is true for the Play flow (AR or practice); the old phases (`scan`, `zones`, `tag`, `align`) still exist for the manual tools (More... > Manual: map my room, Menu > Name spots / Line up / Scan again from scratch). In the Play flow `G.phase` is `play` almost always.

- **Frame loop.** `autoTick(dt)` every frame: scan cadence (0.9 s, 2.2 s once steady, skipped if no new hits): `closeHoles` on dirty bands, `extractZones`, `markBumps`, then (not returning) `liveSync` + `autoSettle`, repaint, count / hint. Then `relocStep` (time-sliced generator, 7 ms per frame), change detection after a lock (`autoCompare`, 25 s after the lock, then every 60 s if 300+ new hits), autosave (10 s), COCO (2 s), the occasional question (`autoAskTick`, 6 s poll, 2 minute gap), the recorder (5 s).
- **Live map** (`liveSync`): a scan zone of 0.05 m2 or more that has been stable for 2 ticks (or is 0.4 m2) becomes a map zone (`newMapZone` + `reconcileLand(first)`); known zones grow (`growLand`, `liveGain`: only adds cells and objects, never removes or reports; removal / tidy detection is only in `captureScan` after a lock); `liveReclass` renames zones when the scan learns more (a desk looks like a floor until the floor is seen). `autoSettle`: biggest clear area (`st===1` cells), x1.25 for 45 cm to 1.1 m above the lowest zone, at least 0.08 m2, a few seconds of looking around unless one is 0.25 m2 already.
- **Saving.** `persist()` is the single save point (never while `G.returning && !G.locked`: the map is still in the old frame and the colony waits). `safeWrite` keeps the previous good JSON in `<key>-bak` and reads the value back; `safeRead` falls back to the backup and sets `STORE.status = 'corrupt'` when nothing is readable; `STORE.status = 'unavailable'` when localStorage does not work. Both are surfaced on the title. `persistSoon()` (1.5 s debounce) is wired by wrapping `addSpot`, `removeSpot`, `giveRequest`, `makeLink`, `applyChanges`, `answerGuess`, `libAdd`, `answerAsk`, `plantTree` at the end of the script (so keep those as plain global functions). Schema: map `v:1`, colony `v:2`; loaders check `v` (a v3 migration goes in `loadSave` / `mapFromJSON`). The map also carries `visits` and per-cell `conf` (visits in which the cell was seen, 0 to 15, one increment per visit: `bumpConf`).
- **Welcome back.** `updateTitle` shows the away time; `previewReturn` loads the saved map and colony, runs `awaySim` with a **seeded** `Math.random` (`withSeed(sv.t, ...)`) and `G.previewSecs` (whole minutes), takes the first 3 diary lines and resets the world. Pressing Play runs the same simulation with the same seed (while the preview is under 2 minutes old), so the title preview and the in-game story are identical. The diary of a returning AR session is held in `G.pendingDiary` and shown 2.5 s after the lock (`relocLock`), buildings created while away pop in (`t.id > G.awayUid0`).
- **Relocalisation** (`RELOC`, all tunables in `RELOC.cfg`). Saved map -> `relocSavedLayers` (clear cells at the zone height, clutter cells at their top height, clustered to layers), live scan -> `relocLiveLayers` (bands). Layers are 4 cm occupancy sets with boundary points. `relocDyCands` pairs layer heights and ranks height offsets by structure agreement (floor + desk + shelf heights). `relocJob`: for each dy (top 4) and yaw (2 degree steps) boundary points of height-compatible layers vote for the shift in an Int16 accumulator (5 cm bins, +-8 m); 8 peaks (non-maximum suppression) are refined by `relocIcp` from five starting yaws (live -> saved nearest boundary points, trimmed Kabsch, 20 rounds with shrinking threshold) and the lowest `relocCost` wins; `relocVerify` on the topmost-height rasters counts matches, conflicts and **informative** cells (next to a real height step). Score = informative agreement minus 3x informative conflicts (+ a little for flat agreement), so a big flat floor cannot win alone. Lock needs: >= 150 matched cells, ratio >= 0.8, >= 30 informative matches, second-best pose scores <= 0.75 of the best, and the last 3 attempts (spanning >= 2 s of scanning) agreeing within 4 degrees, 8 cm, 3 cm. A job takes about 0.1 to 0.4 s of CPU on a desktop (time-sliced). After `RELOC.cfg.askAfter` (60) scan seconds without a lock: `relocAsk`. `RELOC.cands` holds the last candidate table (debugging). `worldMapHook` below.
- **Known limit**: a big flat floor leaves the yaw valley shallow: expect about 1 degree and 2 to 4 cm, not better. The lock is applied once; it is not refined later.
- **Future hook** `worldMapAvailable` / `worldMapSave` / `worldMapLoad` (next to `relocNewRoom`): when the page runs in our own app and the WebXRKit polyfill exposes `session.nonStandard_getWorldMap()` / `nonStandard_setWorldMap()` (webxr-polyfill.js around lines 5795 to 5815), the blob is stored under `base-camp-worldmap` and `startAuto` locks immediately (`relocLock(identity, 'worldmap')`) after `setWorldMap` succeeds. Nothing native is implemented; without the API the surface matcher is used. (Calling `worldMapSave` from `persist` is not wired yet: do that together with the native side.)
- **Recognition** in the Play flow: `recogPropose` (auto branch) turns detections with score >= 0.4 and (2 sightings or >= 0.6) into spots with `src:'auto'`, `n` (confirmations), `cls`; `recogConsider` raises `n` for the same class again (every 20 s at most) and forgets an auto spot when a different class is seen with >= 0.6. `unknownTops` + `autoAskTick` ask for things within `plotR + 0.3` of a colony. `defaultTagFor` gives small tops a default tag by height and size.
- **Wonders** (`wondersTick`, `makeWonder`): a band of 10 to 400 cells at least 1.6 m above the lowest big surface; spot `src:'wonder'` (tag `other`), a toast, a diary line, a recorder entry, a snapshot. Extend `WONDERS`.
- **Field recorder** (`REC`, keys `bc-rec-v1`, `bc-reports-v1`): ring buffers for zones, coverage, recognition, tags, unknown things, homing attempts, frame times, errors (`error` / `unhandledrejection`), non-horizontal hits per 30 cm cell (`recNonHoriz`, called from `arFrame` where such hits used to be dropped). `recSnap(reason)` grabs a <= 640 px JPEG (quality 0.6) of the camera frame, at most 6, one per reason per 20 s, only when `camera-access` frames exist. `buildReport(note)` -> one JSON (`kind: base-camp-field-report`, `v:1`); `reportUi` (Menu > Report) downloads it (Blob link) or copies it (clipboard API, textarea + `execCommand` fallback); the last 5 are kept in storage (older ones lose their pictures if storage is full). Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Build 6: scanning quality (read this before touching SCAN)
- **Why**: the owner's field test merged a speaker top and a shelf of the same height into one zone and drew outlines into thin air. WebXRKit's native `performHitTest` raycasts existing plane geometry and FALLS BACK to estimated planes; the JS polyfill runs one such hit test per XR hit-test source per frame and drops the plane `uuid` (only present for a real ARPlaneAnchor). Estimated-plane hits are the guesses beyond real edges.
- **Direct raycasts** (`DIRECT`, `directInit` / `directFrame` / `directHit` in the AR section): the polyfill device is found through the session's private symbols (`device._arKitWrapper`); each XR frame a rotating subset (9) of a 9 x 7 screen grid (inner 90%, jittered) goes out through `wrapper.hitTest(x, y, 32)`, at most 12 in flight (self-throttling); `world_transform` is converted with `refSpace._inverseBaseMatrix` (the polyfill's local space is the first camera pose, not ARKit world). A one-time check compares a direct (0.5, 0.5) hit with the XR source's centre hit (`DIRECT.chk`: ok / bad); `bad` falls back to the XR sources, `ok` cancels them (less native load). No wrapper (Chrome, Android) = the XR sources as before. `?direct=0` forces the sources. `DIRECT.hps` is the effective hits per second (also in the field report cover entries).
- **Trust** (`scanHit(p, src, kind, uid)`, kind 2 = certain (practice room), 1 = trusted (uuid / WebXR), 0 = weak (estimated, no uuid)). Per band and cell: `na` trusted hits (distinct frames), `ne` weak hits, `kn` known. A cell is KNOWN when `na >= 2` or (`na >= 1` and `na + ne >= 2`): weak hits never create bands, heights, or cells alone (they only attach to an existing band within 3.5 cm). `refineBand(b)` (called by `extractZones` and the tick) turns `kn` into the outline mask `g`: known cells with fewer than 2 known neighbours are dropped (despeckle), then `closeHoles` with `CLOSE_R = 1.5` cells (was 2.5; a 15 cm gap never closes). Everything downstream (zones, land, relocalisation) reads `g`, so it only sees confident cells. `b.uu` keeps the plane anchor id per cell.
- **Zones** (`extractZones`): connected components, 8 neighbours, plus a single empty cell does not split a surface (unless two different plane anchors meet there). There is no distance based merge any more: two same-height surfaces are one zone only if their confident cells connect.
- **Resolution**: kept at 2 cm cells (250 x 250 per band). Cleaner confidence was preferred over smaller cells; 1.5 cm would be 333 x 333 (1.8x memory and chamfer/flood time) and was not tried.
- **Homing records**: every attempt in the field report has score, runner-up score (`second`), overlap, informative cells, yaw / translation / dy of the best, trusted / weak hit counts and a `why` list of the thresholds it missed. The hint while searching reads "Looking for home... NN% (s)" (`RELOC.progShown`: the worst of M / ratio / info / overlap against their thresholds). Thresholds were NOT changed (no real data yet).
- **UI**: in AR the card is one tiny line in the top-right corner (`body.inar #card`, name, settlers, time, temperature).
- **Tests**: `tests/scan-quality-test.js` (two same-height desks 15 cm apart; run against build 5 with `BC_ROOT=<copy>`); `ar-fake.js` returns a plane uuid for real surfaces and uuid-less hits for rays that miss but hit a surface extended by 35 cm (`window.__native.ext`, `.estimated`), plus noise.
- **Rooms (build 7, ON) and the totem (still OFF)**: two flags in index.html. `MULTI_ROOM = true` turns on several rooms: the `HOMES` registry (`bc-rooms-v1`; rows `{id, name, visits, known, cols, last}`), per-room storage keys (`homeKeys`: room `r1` keeps the old keys `base-camp-map-v1` / `base-camp-save-v2`, the others get `.rN`), the rooms list on the title (`roomsUi`: Continue, Rename (inline input, Enter or Save), Delete (two taps, keeps nothing), `+ New room`), `playRoom(want, id)` ('find' = the Play button, 'room' = a row's Continue, 'new'), silent recognition across rooms, and the storage report / bounds (`storeChars`, `roomChars`, `roomBound`: one room is held under 1.4 M characters by dropping its `-bak` copies, near 80% of the assumed 5 M limit the other rooms' `-bak` copies go, a warning toast at 70%). `HOME_TOTEM = false` keeps the totem photo, guardian, FEAT matcher, `relocTotemJob` and `homesUi` unreachable: the previous builder warned the matcher was never validated. Do not turn HOME_TOTEM on without running tests/totem-test.js to the end.
- **Migration** (`homesMigrate`, runs at every start, idempotent): no registry yet means the saved single room is `r1` ("Room 1", or the name saved in the map if it has one); a `-prev` backup (from "Start a new room" / "A new room" in builds 5-6b) is copied to a new room's keys and named "Room N (earlier)", and only then are the `-prev` keys removed (a failed copy leaves them for next time). Build 5/6/6b saves are the same schema (map v1, colony v2), so nothing else is converted. Rooms that were started and never saved anything are dropped when the title shows (`roomsTidy`).
- **Recognition across rooms** (`startAuto`, `relocStep`, `relocPickRoom`, `relocConsider`): Play with two or more saved rooms sets `G.finding`. The most recently used room loads (hidden); the others are kept as layers in `ROOMPRE`. Every room is compared against the fresh scan with the same `relocJob` and the same thresholds as one room (rounds robin, then the leader gets two of three tries); a room locks only when its three stable attempts pass, every other room had at least two attempts, and no other room scores above 0.8 of the winner (the runner-up margin). The winning room is loaded with `homeSwitchLoad` and `relocLock` runs as before. It is SILENT: no text, no percentages, only a small pulsing dot (`#idDot`) in the corner; the numbers live in the field report (`recReloc`, `room`, `rp`). After 45 s of scanning without a lock ONE small prompt (`#nroom`: "New room? [Yes] [It's... v]") appears and the search carries on behind it; Yes = `relocNewRoom` (creates the next room, the old ones are not touched), "It's the Shed" = that room loads and the older two-tap alignment (`relocManual`) takes over. With one room the older flow is unchanged (60 s, the `#ask` card, "Things moved"); only "A new room" now creates a second room instead of writing a `-prev` copy.

## Frames and the one idea that makes returning work
World = AR local space in metres. Every map zone has a frame `(cx, cz, ang, y)`; its land grid, its settlement (`S.o` = origin in the zone's own axes, `S.adj`) and the settlement's contents are all stored **in the zone's frame**. So a returning colony needs only **one** thing: the transform from the saved room to the live room, applied to zone frames and spot positions (`applyAlign`). Settlements follow through `syncTf`. The map is saved again after a visit in the *live* frame (each visit re-anchors it), so errors do not pile up on one origin.

## Room map (key `base-camp-room-map-v1`, JSON, `v: 1`)
```
{ v:1, kind:"base-camp-room-map", name, saved, build, nextMZ, nextSpot, nextCable, music,
  zones:[{ id, name, type FLOOR|TABLE|SHELF|SILL|SMALL, spec, y, h, cx, cz, ang, w, d, area, color, lost,
           land:{ cell:0.02, i0, j0, w, h,            // cells in the zone's own axes: u=(i+.5)*cell, v=(j+.5)*cell
                  st:[v,count,...], dist:[...], calm:[...], ht:[...] } }],  // run-length: 0 off surface / 1 clear / 2 clutter; disturbance 0..15; calm visits 0..15; clutter height in cm
  spots:[{ id, tag, name, zid, x, y, z, area, on:"top"|"obj", src:"player"|"default"|"library", who }],
  cables:[{ id, a, b }],  landmarks:[spotId, spotId],
  library:[{ id, key, tag, name, by, how, at, n }] }
```
Role, travel method and funny things are **not** stored: they come from the tag via `TAGS` + the council's `rooms.json` at runtime, so editing the council file changes the game. Import validates kind, version, sizes (refuses with a message); the colony is never in the map. Export / import is the "Room map" window (text box, download, copy, load file).

## Colony save (key `base-camp-save-v2`)
`{ v:2, t, hour, simT, uid, nameIdx, tidy, settlements:[{ id, zid (map zone), spec, adj, o:[u,v], stock{wood food seeds water tin}, stats, things[], cols[] }], links[] }`. `things` includes landmarks (`kind:'lm'`, `role`, `spot`), rubble, stations. The prototype's v1 save is no longer read.

## Spot roles (what each role does; `roleStep`, `fxOf`)
tower: morale while Music is on (the model shakes). well: water. silo: cap + 12. storage: caps + 24. plaza / lookout: slow morale. forge: tin every 10 min (and warm refuge: cancels the cold growth penalty). solar: growth x1.2 and fruit. farm: +1 farm, ripens x1.25. workshop: builds x1.3. hideout: arrivals every 60 s instead of 100. market: 3 food -> 1 seed + 1 wood every 20 min. Zones get a **default spot** (floor -> farm, desk -> market, shelf -> storage, sill -> solar...), so a plain scan already has roles. Tin counts double towards building cost (`canPay` / `pay`); huts bought with tin get a grey roof.

## Away sim (`awaySim`)
`simSettlement` in quiet mode with `E = AWAYE` (`away: true`): coarse steps of `clamp(secs/1500, 20, 60)` seconds, at most 24 h, day/night from sunrise/sunset (no weather), jobs run with the real step (a job is two ticks). Colonies keep needs at 0.3x while asleep. Extras: council events at random hours, `autoExpand` (a thriving colony sends a party over a spot's travel method to an unsettled zone), cables marked on the map start gated (`l.gate`: four settlers). The diary (`awaySummary`) diffs before / after and picks 5 to 10 lines. Runs on the saved map **before** any scan or alignment (settlements are hidden, `placed:false`, via `G.holdPlace`).

## Alignment maths
Map -> live: `p' = R(phi) p + t`, `y' = y + dy`, `R(phi)(x, z) = (x cos phi - z sin phi, x sin phi + z cos phi)`. Two landmarks `a, b` (saved) and `a', b'` (tapped): `phi = atan2(b'-a') - atan2(b-a)`, `t = mid' - R(phi) mid`, `dy = mean of the two height differences`. Residual `err = max |R(phi) p + t - p'|` (half the length mismatch). Landmarks are the two **player-tagged** small spots that are furthest apart (`pickLandmarks`); a tap snaps to the centre of the tapped patch (`surfacePick`) so repeated taps agree. Fine tuning rotates about the landmark midpoint (`nudgeAlign`). One landmark: shift only. None: `Same place`.

## Change detection (`captureScan`, `applyChanges`)
1. Fresh scan zones are matched to map zones: big surfaces by overlap (many patches may belong to one surface; the land grid grows with `growLand`), small things by "mostly on a known thing" (`fzOnZone`) or close (15 cm). A small top over known land is an object standing there (clutter), not new land.
2. Unmatched small map zone that was **looked at** (6+ direct hits on a *lower* band around it, almost none at its own height): MOVED if a similar unmatched fresh zone (height +-4.5 cm, area x0.55..1.8, within 1.3 m) exists, else GONE. Not looked at: untouched.
3. Unmatched fresh zones: NEW (expedition request).
4. Per matched zone `measureLand` -> `reconcileLand`: changes are whole objects (cells eroded by one so scan jitter makes no events, regrown by one). *Removed* needs **direct hit evidence** (`b.hit`) over the object's cells, so sparse sweeps never look like tidying. Events: removed object = gift by size (`objClass`: can < 0.022 m2, box < 0.09, else big), placed object on clear land = squash check against every thing's footprint, disturbance +1 on the cells (dilated 2), calm reset; all other cells calm +1.
5. Learning: planner (`validSpot`) refuses cells with disturbance >= 2 (`TIDY.avoidAt`); placement prefers cells with the most calm visits; `calm >= 3` marks a thing `rich` (x1.3 growth).
6. Spots standing on an object that disappeared: a similar object appeared nearby -> *"Did the cup move here?"*; otherwise the spot is dropped with a diary line. A moved zone takes its removal / placement events with it (not tidying or squashing).
Thresholds all live in `TIDY` and a few literals in `captureScan`.

## Recognition (phase 2)
- **Frames**: AR session requests `camera-access` only when mapping; the polyfill puts the JPEG in the device's `_cameraImage` (`arDevice()` finds it through the session's private symbol, like the overlay fix). `recogFrame()` returns it with its size. Detection boxes are mapped to the screen by proportion (**assumes the app sends a frame that has the screen's field of view and orientation; check on a real iPad**).
- **COCO-SSD** (`lite_mobilenet_v2`, TF.js 4.22.0, coco-ssd 2.2.3 from jsdelivr, weights from storage.googleapis.com), loaded when AR mapping starts, run about every 1.5 s while scanning or tagging. Bottom-centre of the box -> `surfacePick` -> nearest small top (`topNear`) -> candidate; two sightings (or 60% sure) -> a guess card. `COCO_ROLES` is the class table.
- **CLIP** (opt-in): `clip-labels.json` holds normalised text embeddings (43 labels, 512 dims, "a photo of {label}", made by `tests/make-clip-labels.mjs` with `text_model` q8) plus four test-only `checks`. The game downloads only `Xenova/clip-vit-base-patch32` **vision** (q4f16 about 53 MB; falls back through q4, bnb4, q8 if a device cannot run it). Crop of the tapped thing -> embedding -> dot product x100 -> softmax; at 50% it asks "I think this is...", below that "What is this?" with the top three as hints. The toggle is remembered (`bc-clip`).
- **Library** (`G.library`, saved in the map): keys `coco:<class>`, `clip:<label>`, `spot:<id>`. A library entry replaces the guess and naming happens without a question. Practice room: `practiceDetector` (a cup is sure, cans look like a bottle, the GPU box looks like a laptop, speaker and box are unknown).
- Privacy: frames are only given to local models; nothing is uploaded.

## The council's data
`almanac.json` (the council's shape: colonists with free-text personality, `requests {colonist, asks_for, says}`, `events {title, when, weather, what_happens, colonist_says}`) is adapted in `adoptAlmanac`: numeric personality from the name and a few keywords, free-text likes -> building kinds, `asks_for` -> our request conditions. The council's requests come first (a request with a `who` only fires for that colonist), built-in ones are the fallback. `rooms.json` (spots with `used_for`, `gives`, `costs`, `how_they_get_there`, `funny_thing`, `by`) feeds roles, travel names, funny diary lines and the spot card. `creatures.json` (Glip, Grizzle, Fluffflur, Glimmerfluff) are the fourth to seventh... settlers (after the six almanac colonists) and every colonist gets a creature **temperament**: `says_when_it_panics` (squashing, gone things), `says_when_happy` (gifts, arrivals). "by <resident>" is shown small next to every line that uses the council's words. Missing or broken files: built-in copies. Files are fetched next to the page (not from `file:`).

## Known bugs / rough edges
- **Change detection after a lock is conservative on purpose**: a cell only counts as evidence of tidying or squashing when two earlier visits saw it, so those events start on the third visit. With a thin first map (sparse sweeps inflate the tops of cans by a few cm) the earlier rule invented gifts and squashed huts after every return in the fake shed. A real iPad with denser hits may allow this to be relaxed (`conf0` in `reconcileLand`).
- The lock is only as exact as the scan: a big flat floor leaves the yaw valley shallow (see Relocalisation); the lock is polished for 24 s on the clean outlines of tops (`relocPolish`) and then committed. If a room has no tops (just a floor) the match needs a long time or never reaches the information threshold (60 informative cells): it falls back to the question after 60 s.
- The relocation question counts scan seconds (hits arriving), not wall time; "Keep looking" disables the question for the rest of the session.
- The away story is seeded with the save time, so the same save always tells the same story (a feature: the title preview equals the in-game diary); a different save gives a different one.
- Camera snapshots are kept in memory only (a reloaded page loses them); reports built earlier keep theirs.
- With sparse real sweeps a small top can still be mis-reported: in 1 of 3 real-sweep AR runs a can-sized top that the second sweep only half covered came out as "New land! The can is clear" plus "New land spotted" (the other two runs reported nothing). `ar-room-test.js` prints the changes but does not assert on them for that reason. Likely fixes: require direct hits over most of the footprint before believing a removal, and ask "Did the can go?" instead of announcing it.
- A scan only covers 5 m x 5 m around where it started (`GN`); bigger rooms need a bigger grid.
- Colonists can stand in each other's way; they steer around clutter by local avoidance only and can get stuck in a dead end for a few seconds.
- (fixed in build 6) Two surfaces at the same height used to merge; now only connected confident cells form a zone.
- A very small spot moved less than 15 cm is not reported (it is treated as the same).
- Away colonies that got no care end up glum (requests wait for the Giant); the card shows it, nothing worse happens (colonists never die).
- Zone classification is rule based; a stool seat or a chair may be called a Desk.
- Practice-room orbit uses mouse drag only.
