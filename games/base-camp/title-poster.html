Put the Base Camp poster on a TV or a monitor that stays in one place. The room's sticker is hidden in the picture, so while the poster is up the iPad can find the room.

2. Make it the right size. Hold a bank card flat against the screen over the dashed box. Press − or + until the box is exactly the size of the card. (The screen sticker page uses the same size, so do it once on each screen.)

Then: turn off the screen saver and sleep. In the game, find the camp first, then look at the poster: the room keeps that sticker. Next time, point the iPad at the poster and the camp jumps straight back. Tap or press a key to come back here.
