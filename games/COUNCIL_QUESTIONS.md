# Question pack for the PatchTown council: Baguettes & Ballistics

Written 11 Nov 2026 by CAIRN in a cloud session that could not reach PatchTown.
Run it in the next session on Harry's Mac, using the `consult-the-council` skill.

## How to run it

1. Give each resident the **Context** below, then the questions **one at a time**.
2. Ask for their own ideas. Do not show them CAIRN's or Harry's ideas first, so the answers stay theirs.
3. Ask for short answers: up to 3 ideas per question, one line each, plus one line on why.
4. **Count who actually spoke.** For every question, record how many offices gave a real answer, and how many came back empty, off-topic or broken. A council where most turns are empty is not advice.
5. Save the raw answers and the count in `games/COUNCIL_ANSWERS.md` (template at the bottom). Do not tidy the residents' words.
6. Harry chooses what to use. CAIRN builds it and credits the resident whose idea it was in the commit message.

## Context (give this to every resident)

Baguettes & Ballistics is a toy-car war game for the iPad. Tiny toy cars race and fight on a kitchen worktop:
cereal is the money, dominoes knock down bases, a gumball machine gives prizes, and the villain is Baron von Baguette
(a posh baguette with a monocle). Before each battle there is a race through a toy kitchen (the Kitchen Run).

It now works in **AR** (augmented reality): you point the iPad at your real kitchen, and the toy cars race on your
actual worktop through the camera.

Being built right now:
- **My Track:** you scan your real table with the iPad, then either draw a race track with your finger, or press
  Auto and the game builds one for you. Then you race on it, and the battle happens on your real table.
- **Midnight Kitchen Drift:** the kitchen at night with neon lights. You hold a DRIFT button to slide the toy car
  sideways round corners, and score points for long, fast, wide drifts chained into combos.

## The questions

**Q1. My Track.** What makes a home-made race track fun to drive? What should the Auto button do so that the track
it builds surprises you?

**Q2. Drift.** What makes drifting feel great on a touch screen, when you can't feel a steering wheel? What should
the player see and hear during a really good drift?

**Q3. AR.** What could happen in a real kitchen through the camera that could never happen on a normal screen?

**Q4. The cast.** What would Baron von Baguette do in a drift battle? Give him one move, one line of trash talk,
and one weakness.

**Q5. Open.** What one thing would make you want to show this game to a friend?

**Q6. Toy boxes (the big one).** Each player picks a toy box at the start. It is their home base, it unfolds into a
little playset (like the old fold-out toy-car carry cases), it goes with them through the whole game and gets
upgraded, and in multiplayer everyone brings their own. It should feel like a collector's item.
Invent ONE toy box. Give:
- what everyday kitchen or household object it is, and who owns it;
- how it opens or unfolds (the moment it clunks open is the showpiece);
- its special ability in battle;
- its weakness (every box must have one);
- how it plays differently (attacker, defender, helper, sneaky, fast...);
- one upgrade you would save up for;
- the sound it makes when it opens.
