# Micro Precinct

A Micro Machines-style toy war on a kitchen table, built to play on an iPad.
Precinct Assault rules: capture bottle caps to earn bolts, build and upgrade
turrets, send drones, unlock vehicles at your toy box, and smash the red one.

- **Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js r128 from cdnjs).
- **Touch:** drag on the left half to drive; hold FIRE; Gun, Rocket, Upgrade,
  Drones on the right; the garage appears when you drive home.
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrows, Space to fire, 1 gun, 2 rocket, U upgrade,
  3 drones, 4–9 vehicles at home.

Vehicles: Racer and Tank from the start; Hover (drifts, floats over juice and
cereal), Mech (builds turrets at half price), Monster (rams), Jet Bike (fastest)
unlock with bolts.
