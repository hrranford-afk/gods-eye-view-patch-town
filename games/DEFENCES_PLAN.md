# Defences and upkeep: plan v0 (20 Nov 2026)

Harry: "we make bases but not defences. How you maintain your guns, placing traps. Look at Clash of Clans, pick a few strong ideas."
Rule: MECHANICS ONLY. No names, characters, art, layouts, music or look copied. Our world: mice, planks, rope, tin. Nobody dies. Toy weapons only.

What the research showed (public fan guides): defences are a mix of auto-firing towers with different ranges and targets; hidden traps; walls that split a base into compartments to slow attackers; a lure that pulls attackers into a kill zone; layers that cover each other; upgrades.

## Six ideas for our game (ranked)
1. **Auto-defences with a crew mouse.** Pop-gun post (fast, short range, cork pellets that knock parts off a Marble's mice-free path... = shoots incoming rolling shots / knocks the attacker's squad back), Slinger post (slow, long range, lobs). A post only fires while its crew mouse is on it and it has ammo.
2. **Upkeep (Harry's "maintain your guns").** Every post has ammo and a jam meter. Between rounds you restock and oil: spend build points, or send a mouse (uses the mender system). An unattended gun jams after N shots. This makes the build budget a real choice: more guns or more upkeep.
3. **Hidden traps.** Spring plank, glue puddle, rolling log, trip cord, snap board. Hidden from the raider until triggered; the builder sees them. One-use, then they need resetting (upkeep again). Placement matters: cover the gaps and gates.
4. **Compartments.** Inner walls split a camp into rooms; each room has a gate and holds a post or the Keep. Attackers must break through each. Uses today's wall/gate support rules.
5. **Cover and range holes.** Every post has a minimum and a maximum range, so you must layer short and long posts to cover each other, and a lobber post cannot hit what is right under it.
6. **Bait.** A decoy flag or lantern that raiders go for first, placed to draw them into a trap corner.

## Order of build
A. Auto-defences + upkeep (ideas 1 and 2), in the Lab, as pieces with parameters (like the Weapon Workshop).
B. Hidden traps (3), Lab.
C. Compartments (4) as camp templates in the Lab.
D. Raid a base mode (RAID_MODE_PLAN.md) using all of the above, then bring to matches after re-proof.
E. Bait and range holes (5, 6) last.
