# Dragon defence: co-op idea (Harry, 16 Nov 2026)

## Harry's words, kept
"What if you and your mate had to shoot down dragons and stuff and build little bases, and you're on the same team? How cool would that be?"

## How it could fit the engine (CAIRN's first notes, not decided)
- Same team, not versus: two players defend bases they built on the real furniture.
- Dragons are our own designs made from household things (sock dragon, tea-towel dragon, paper-kite dragon). No copies of any film or game dragon.
- Nobody dies: a hit dragon goes dizzy, sneezes out its loot and flaps off home; maybe a tamed one joins the team.
- Dragons perch on real furniture, swoop between surfaces, and hide behind the sofa (depth, build 30).
- Bases built piece by piece with kept resources (ties into the mega projects and builders' yard).
- Reuses: Base Camp battle physics and toy weapons, B&B guns, controllers and split screen.

## Ways to play together
1. Now possible: two players on one Mac with two controllers, in the dolls' house (split screen from B&B).
2. Now possible: one iPad in AR plus a mate on a controller (one screen, two players).
3. Later: two iPads in the same real room. Needs a small online service: Harry's money and security call.
