## What to do with it: a menu
**S = small, M = medium.** Each has its maker.

| | Idea | Maker | Why it helps | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Workshop reach.** A workshop only speeds up mending of pieces near it (about three piece-widths). Put it beside your Keep and the Keep mends fast; put it in the corner and it does nothing for the Keep. Raiding the workshop then matters. | Reasoner and Elder (the Workshop and Watchtower loop); Surveyor (raid the workshop) | **Answers "building makes no difference" directly:** where you put things decides what is protected. | S |
| **B** | **Wind.** A small wind arrow on the table. It changes each round and pushes marbles sideways. The aim line shows the push. | Surveyor ("wind direction and strength"), Coder ("Wind Blast") | A real decision before each shot, with no new button. | S |
| **C** | **Sleep dust.** A pouch that stuns every mouse in a small area for 3 seconds. It does no damage. Counters a mending crew or guards. | Surveyor, Reasoner (dust bunnies), Elder and Radeon Coder (confetti) | Gives raiders a way in without breaking things. | S |
| **D** | **Crown mouse.** When a Keep falls, a tiny mouse in a crown pops out of the rubble waving a white flag. | Surveyor | Keeps "nobody dies", and makes the win funny. | S |
| **E** | **Marble Redirector.** A pipe building that catches an incoming marble and sends it back, weaker, towards where it came from. Needs careful placing, and a bad one can bounce a marble into your own camp. | Surveyor | A risky building with a clear payoff. | M |
| **F** | **Tunnel.** Two holes joined underground: mice go in one and come out the other, under a wall. The enemy can find and fill one end. | Elder, Coder, Radeon Coder | A way past walls that is not breaking them. | M |
| **G** | **Net.** An ammo that entangles every mouse in an area for 2 seconds. | Surveyor | A second stun with a different counter. | S |
| **H** | **Bridge to the hill.** A raised plank crossing the open ground. Fast for your mice, and it collapses if hit. | Quick Builder | Risk and reward. | M |
| later | Barracks, supplies, armory: a resource side to the game. | Coder, Elder, Builder and others | Bigger. It would add a whole economy, so it is a separate decision. | L |
| no | Fire, fireballs, firecrackers, deadly traps. | Coder, Quick Builder, Builder | Not toy-safe. If used, they become dizzy-only. | |

## My recommendation (CAIRN)
Build **A, B and D first.** They are small, and between them they answer the two things you said: building should matter, and each shot should be a choice. Then **one** of E or F as the risky building, and **C** as the new ammo. Hold G and H, and the economy.

This keeps the game's rule that nothing is a health bar: each of these works on parts and mice.
