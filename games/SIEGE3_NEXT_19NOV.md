## The Brain's 8 picks from siege-3b (all makers checked by the town)
ChatGPT #8 Elastic-Band Bank (three boosted shots a round) · Grok #3 Seesaw platform (flings repair planks) · ChatGPT #11 Boomerang Marble (a curved gutter swings a marble round a wall) · Grok #8 Clockwork beetles (auto-repair) · Gemini #1 Cheese-Wheel Gyroscope (curves incoming shots) · the Toymaker's toy-factory workshop (mice feed marbles into a slot to charge the launcher) · ChatGPT #2 Snail Post Office (repairs on the move) · Gemini #11 Acorn Drum (rolls down the hill).

The Brain also says to drop three of the 19 Nov picks: Workshop and Watchtower combo, Sleep dust, and Shield generator with Armory ("too passive or abstract"). I agree about the shield and armory. I would keep the workshop idea as a placement rule (Workshop reach below), because that is not passive.

Rules the town checked: thrown out were ChatGPT #15 and #17 (flagged by ChatGPT as look-alikes of existing games), Grok #14 (fire) and Grok #18 (spears). I also drop Grok #17 ("whisper marble" shows hidden mice through walls: not our game), and anything that needs a health bar.

**Small honest note:** the tiny experts got 1 of the 8 picks, and the Brain says the big models were more playable. The tiny experts' charm is the toy texture (the "crinkle", the box that opens to a world). Worth keeping as flavour, not as the rules.
