# Baguettes & Ballistics

*Mealtime Mayhem*: a Micro Machines-style toy war across the kitchen, built
to play on an iPad. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for where it's going.

- **Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js r128 from cdnjs).
- **Touch:** drag on the left half to drive; hold FIRE; build buttons on the
  right; the garage appears when you drive home.
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrows, Space fire, T truck, G digger, 1 gun turret,
  2 rocket turret, U upgrade, 3 drones, 4–9 vehicles at home.

**Loadout Race: the Kitchen Run (every level).** Each level opens with one lap (about 45 to 60 seconds; a perfect run is 40) of an obstacle course through a whole toy kitchen, against three red cars: up a chopping board onto the bread bin and jump the gap, through a kitchen-roll tube (or the risky cutlery-drawer shortcut), up cereal-box ramps to the window sill (or bounce up on the whisk), down a baking-tray slide, over floating sponges in the sink (or along the narrow ruler), up a butter knife into the toaster, which fires you across to the island, round the hob rings (one is hot), over the cake, under the mini fridge and into the microwave: ding, you come out of the oven door at the finish. Fall off and you are back on the road a little way behind. Floating tokens are the tool buttons: drive through one to win that tool. You carry 4 at most (a 5th swaps out your oldest); Gun turret, Dump truck and Digger are always yours, plus one must-have on some levels (Flare on level 3, Gunboat on level 4, Tank squad on level 6). Where you finish sets your starting cereal (1st +40, 2nd +25, 3rd +12, 4th +5) and 1st place gets a free Upgrade. The enemy's best car sets its own limited loadout the same way. A YOUR LOADOUT card then explains each tool in one line, and the battle shows only those buttons (7 to 9 instead of 8 to 13). "Skip race" on the intro gives a ready-made loadout.

**Level select.** The title card has 6 level buttons; pick any level from the start. Stars (win, under 8 minutes, no truck lost) are saved on the device.

**Level 6, Baguettes & Ballistics (the finale, stage 1).** A big night worktop shaped like a U: your toy box at the tip of the sink arm (water, ferries, boats), theirs on the breadboard, and the hob (hot rings, the cook's pan, paperclips) in the bend between. First comes a 60-second build-up while the kettle boils: a glowing ceasefire line splits the bend, nobody crosses or shoots over it, and both sides gather, build turrets and train **squadrons** (Bombers, Boats, Tanks, Raiders; tap a squad's button to set Attack, Defend or Raid; Rally moves where Defend squads wait; hold Peek to look at Red's side). In the last 10 seconds pick a **battle plan** (Left, Middle or Right lane; keys I, O, P): Red secretly picks too, both are revealed when the kettle clicks and the war starts, and a defence that covers the lane Red chose takes 25% less damage there. Baguette bombers fly over water and walls and drop crumb bombs; rocket turrets are the answer. Sugar walls (button or B, 12 cereal) block lanes. **Stage 2:** everything you collected becomes an army. **Infantry** squadrons (Cereal Loopers grab caps and soak fire, Paperclip Pikemen hurt cars and hold a line, Pea Shooters float over the sink and lob over walls, Battery Bots stun turrets and cars) cost peas and batteries from small risky piles on the worktop, and count half a unit toward the cap of 24. At 3:00 of war **the fridge door swings open** (light floods the table for 20 s, then it slams and the kitchen stays darker) and **THE BAGUETTE** rolls out: an armoured bread train on its own rails round the U, with a crusty engine and four carriages (Stapler, Duck, Remote and Toaster), each with its own hp and weapon. Cut the carriages off one by one; only then can the engine be hurt. Its shield stays on their toy box until it dies, unless you set off **the Great Domino Run** (the longest run in the game: from the button at the bottom-left of the hob, along the bend and up the enemy arm, 500 damage to their toy box and it breaks the shield; it needs weight 3 on the button, infantry count half, and sets itself up again after 90 s). Red has a smaller run aimed at you. Smash their toy box to flick the kitchen light on and see **MEALTIME MAYHEM COMPLETE** with your stars for all six levels. **Stage 3, the giant worktop:** level 6 is now 346 x 216, four times the area of a normal level. The sink is a wide pool in the middle of your arm with a land lane down each side, a ruler bridge and two big bottle-cap ferries (boats sail it, pea shooters float across it). A **minimap** (top right, level 6 only) shows the U, the water, both toy boxes, your car and squads, the enemies you can see and the Baguette. Two ready-made **toasters** per side along the route throw you 48 units (and the Toaster button works on this level). Tank squads take about 40 s to cross; the Baguette's track is 857 long and the Great Domino Run has 89 dominoes.

**Level 5, Hob and oven.** The table is a black glass hob with four rings that cool for 10 seconds, glow orange for 3 and then burn red-hot for 6, hurting anything sitting on them (a clock at the top left counts it down). At the back, the oven door lies down as a ramp to a ledge: cars on it are lifted, turrets built there reach 30% further, and the middle cap and the paperclips are up there. Build a toaster launcher (Toaster button or K, 30 cereal) and drive onto it to be popped 30 units ahead over walls and rings; a shadow shows where you will land. The enemy is a rusher: it gathers big waves and sends them when the rings are cool, and at 3:00 the Toaster Tank lobs flaming toast.

**Level 3, Remote-control drawer.** It is dark inside the drawer: you only see what a light touches. Enemy cars hide until your headlights, a turret, a glowing battery pile, the torch or a flare (Flare button or V, 8 cereal) shows them. Batteries pay for Laser turrets (L) and the Jet Bike; the enemy is sneaky (early hijackers, raiders with no siren); at 3:00 the Remote Car races round your toy box and snipes.

**Level 4, The Sink.** A channel of water splits the table: wheels can't cross it except on one ruler bridge or a bottle-cap ferry, and the Hover skims over. Launch soap-dish gunboats (Boat button or B, 30 cereal, three at most); the enemy sends boats and sponge rafts too. The tap drips splash rings that shove cars and boats sideways, a rich cereal pile sits on a soap-dish island, and at 4:00 the Rubber Duck paddles in and lobs bubbles.

**Level 1** used to open with a box race round its own table; it now starts with the Kitchen Run like every level (the box-race code is still in the file, unused).

**Level 2, Pencil case.** Paperclips from the open pencil case pay for rockets and the Tank; shove erasers to block lanes; the enemy raids your trucks every 90 seconds; the Stapler arrives at 4:00.

**Level 1, Breakfast table.** Diggers dig cereal out of spilled bowls, dump
trucks haul it to your toy box or any bottle cap you hold. Spend it on
turrets, drones, trucks and vehicles. Wrecks spill cereal: drive over it to
scoop it up. The middle bowl keeps refilling. The enemy runs the same
economy, so raid its trucks.

**Level 2 extras.** Staple gun turret (Q): staples pin cars in place. Paperclip armour (R, at home): up to 3 plates, each takes 15% off every hit. Magnet (F): pulls in loose cereal and drags enemy cars. Catapult (E): lobs erasers over walls at their toy box from long range.

**Sneaky attacks (every level).** Domino (X): line them up to their toy box and knock the first one. Trojan (Y): next to one of their dump trucks, hijack it and it drives home with a firework. The enemy does both too: shoot their red dominoes from your side to knock the run away, and ram a hijacked truck of yours to defuse it.

**Domino runs on every level.** Drive onto your blue button mid-table to set off your run at their toy box. It's stiff: it needs a Monster truck, or a lighter car plus your drones (the Hover floats and can't press it). They have one too, aimed at yours; it sets itself up again after 45 seconds.

**Gumball Machine (level 2).** Ram it to spin for 10 cereal: power-ups, cereal, a free truck, or items to carry and use with Z (butter, jam, marble, wasp, sherbet fizz). The enemy spins it too.

**Gumball Machine on level 6.** It stands in the middle of the bend, on the ceasefire line (24 cereal a spin, game cereal only). Besides level 2's prizes it can give **heroes** (one alive per side per type): Captain Teaspoon (fast melee, flips infantry, squads near him hit 25% harder), the Salt Shaker (sniper whose salt patches slow enemy cars), Nurse Duck (heals nearby units slowly, fragile) and the Egg Timer (rings every 30 s: your squads get 10 s of speed). About 1 spin in 25 gives **THE STAND MIXER**, a huge walking mixer with two spinning whisks that smashes everything near it, flattens sugar walls and marches on the enemy toy box; only one ever appears in a match, for either side. If Red gets it you get a banner and an arrow. Heroes and the mixer show on the minimap. Red's Great Domino Run button is now in the middle of the bend and Red sends a heavy squad to press it every minute or two.
