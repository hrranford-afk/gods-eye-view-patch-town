# Baguettes & Ballistics

*Mealtime Mayhem*: a Micro Machines-style toy war across the kitchen, built
to play on an iPad. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for where it's going.

- **Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js r128 from cdnjs).
- **Touch:** drag on the left half to drive; hold FIRE; build buttons on the
  right; the garage appears when you drive home.
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrows, Space fire, T truck, G digger, 1 gun turret,
  2 rocket turret, U upgrade, 3 drones, 4–9 vehicles at home.

**Level select.** The title card has 6 level buttons; pick any level from the start. Stars (win, under 8 minutes, no truck lost) are saved on the device, and levels 3, 5 and 6 say "coming soon".

**Level 4, The Sink.** A channel of water splits the table: wheels can't cross it except on one ruler bridge or a bottle-cap ferry, and the Hover skims over. Launch soap-dish gunboats (Boat button or B, 30 cereal, three at most); the enemy sends boats and sponge rafts too. The tap drips splash rings that shove cars and boats sideways, a rich cereal pile sits on a soap-dish island, and at 4:00 the Rubber Duck paddles in and lobs bubbles.

**Level 2, Pencil case.** Paperclips from the open pencil case pay for rockets and the Tank; shove erasers to block lanes; the enemy raids your trucks every 90 seconds; the Stapler arrives at 4:00.

**Level 1, Breakfast table.** Diggers dig cereal out of spilled bowls, dump
trucks haul it to your toy box or any bottle cap you hold. Spend it on
turrets, drones, trucks and vehicles. Wrecks spill cereal: drive over it to
scoop it up. The middle bowl keeps refilling. The enemy runs the same
economy, so raid its trucks.

**Level 2 extras.** Staple gun turret (Q): staples pin cars in place. Paperclip armour (R, at home): up to 3 plates, each takes 15% off every hit. Magnet (F): pulls in loose cereal and drags enemy cars. Catapult (E): lobs erasers over walls at their toy box from long range.

**Sneaky attacks (every level).** Domino (X): line them up to their toy box and knock the first one. Trojan (Y): next to one of their dump trucks, hijack it and it drives home with a firework. The enemy does both too: shoot their red dominoes from your side to knock the run away, and ram a hijacked truck of yours to defuse it.

**Domino runs on every level.** Drive onto your blue button mid-table to set off your run at their toy box. It's stiff: it needs a Monster truck, or a lighter car plus your drones (the Hover floats and can't press it). They have one too, aimed at yours; it sets itself up again after 45 seconds.
