# Base Camp build 20: replay of the first 10 minutes

**How I played.** Live build 20. Fresh browser, no save, iPad size (820 x 1180). Two runs of 10 real minutes.
- **Run A:** real clock. It was 01:02, so night.
- **Run B:** Hour slider set to 13:00, so day.

I followed every card and tapped what glowed. Shots are in `replay20/`. I logged every sound the game played.

---

## Better than before

1. **The camera finds the camp.** Build 19 showed a chair. Now "We landed!" shows the tent and three colonists, big and central (`A01`, `B01`).
2. **Pip asks for help.** At 0:35 in both runs, one card: "My seeds spilled!" The Seeds button gets a yellow ring (`A02`, `B02`). Build 19 had zero requests.
3. **The first seed works.** My first tap by the tent planted it. Sounds: plant, cheer, fireworks. Build 19 said "No room there."
4. **Night has a campfire.** The colonists sit round a fire, awake, by the tent (`A03` to `A06`). Nobody goes to bed.
5. **The weather card waits.** It now comes at 0:51, after Pip's card, not at 0:09 on top of it.
6. **Minute 9 has a cliffhanger.** At 9:03 Pip says: "Something glitters over on the Sunny Perch. I will go and look tonight. Come back later." This is the proposed Beat 8.
7. **The promise is kept.** After coming back: "found what glittered: a lost button, shiny as a moon. It sits by the campfire now."
8. **Sounds are real.** Landing: founded, pop, fanfare, fireworks. Pip's card: an "ask" chime. Seed: plant and cheer.
9. **The day run is lively.** First hut 1:31. Well 1:36. Lanterns, leaf pile, farm. Luna joins at 2:38. A tree by 8:30 (`B05`, `B06`).

---

## Still weak or new problems

1. **Night is still dead.** The campfire is pretty, but nothing happens. From 1:07 to 9:00, `A03` to `A06` look the same. No building. The seed does not sprout. The only sounds in those 8 minutes were one river fanfare at 4:00. A child who starts after dinner still gets 8 still minutes.
2. **"Come back later" is a shock.** In the practice room it skips 8 hours at once. 30 to 40 new settlers. 28 huts. New colonies on the desk. The camera jumps to a dark overview (`A07`). The child's tiny camp is gone.
3. **Five voices at once after the return.** "We're home!", "First hut", "Ten colonists", a "No room to build" card, and a long "While you were away" list (`B07`). The list has 10 bullets. It covers half the screen at minute 10 (`B08`).
4. **Grown-up text leaks.** "by The Dell Reasoner", "by The Town Brain" under kid lines. One toast says "by CAIRN (placeholder)". Also "plan a advent calendar". Also "Nobody shared nicely."
5. **Clone names.** "Gertie 2", "Pip the Packet 2", "Glimmerfluff 3" on the camera buttons.
6. **Two popups at once.** At 2:45 "What is this? A small top, 20 cm across, 0.16 m up" sat on top of the Build menu (`B04`). A child will not know what "0.16 m up" means.
7. **The sky panel is still on screen.** "Practice sky" sits bottom right in every shot. It covers the end of the Build menu (`B06`).
8. **Build is mostly locked.** Hut needs 5 wood. I had 1. The first tap on Build says no.
9. **Trees hide the camp.** The planted tree grows right in front of the tent and a colonist (`B05`, `B06`).
10. **Day sound is busy.** About 1,100 sounds in 10 minutes, nearly 2 a second. Mostly footsteps and hammers. 77 were dropped by the limiter. It may tire the ear.
11. **Slider bug is still there.** Setting the hour gave "First night survived" at 0:15 in daylight.
12. **One ask only.** After the seeds, nobody asked me for anything until minute 9.

---

## Score: would a 9-year-old want to come back tomorrow?

- **Build 19: 3 / 10.** Lost camera, no job, then 8 minutes of nothing.
- **Build 20: 5 / 10.** They get a job and a mystery to come back for, but night is still still, and the return buries the camp in text and strangers.

By day alone I would give build 20 a 6.

## The next 3 changes

1. **Give the night campfire things to do. (M)**
   - The seed sprouts at night too, within a minute.
   - One more ask at about minute 3: "Gertie needs 2 sticks for the fire."
   - A small night event at about minute 6: a shooting star, a song round the fire, a moth visit.
   - Pip's river bridge also fires at night.
2. **Make coming back gentle. (M)**

   - Keep the camera on the old camp.
   - Show the answer first: "Pip found a button!" Then max 3 short lines. Hide the rest behind "More".
   - One banner at a time. Queue the rest.
   - No "2" and "3" on names.
3. **Clean the screen for kids. (S)**
   - Hide "Practice sky" behind the Menu.
   - Remove "by ..." credits and the "placeholder" text from kid lines.
   - Hold the "What is this?" quiz until the second visit, and say it simply: "What is this thing?"
   - Fix "a advent". Fix the slider "First night" banner.
   - Let the first hut cost what the camp has, or gift the wood with a "Give" card.
