## Round 3: Base Camp battles (a turn-based, Worms-like mode)

Context for every resident: Base Camp is an AR game where tiny creatures (Glip, Grizzle, Gloop, Glimmerfluff, Pip,
Pip the Mapmaker and friends) live on the real surfaces of a room: speaker tops, shelves, the floor, the worktop.
A new mode: two rival colonies on different surfaces of the same real room take turns lobbing things at each other
in arcs, a bit like the classic turn-based artillery games. It must stay funny and kind: nobody dies; a hit creature
pops into fluff and is "sent home" to its tent. Players can be two people passing one iPad, or the player against a
rival colony.

**B1. The rivals.** Who is the rival colony that lives in the room and wants your land? Give them a name, what they
are made of, where they live in a real room, and why they fight (something silly).

**B2. Weapons from household rubbish.** Invent three weapons the creatures build from things found in a room
(bottle caps, elastic bands, paperclips, tea bags, drawing pins, crumbs, string). For each: what it is, how it flies
(arc, bounce, roll, sticky, cluster), what it does when it lands, and its funny side effect.

**B3. The room is the battlefield.** How should real surfaces change the fight? (Shooting up to a high shelf, a
cable car as a moving target, a speaker that shakes everything when music plays, the floor as no-man's-land.)

**B4. Big moments.** One "super weapon" for a whole match (e.g. calling in the Hoover) and how it is earned.

**B5. Winning and losing kindly.** How does a match end, what does the winner get, and how does the loser feel okay?

**B6. One rule that makes it unlike other artillery games.**
