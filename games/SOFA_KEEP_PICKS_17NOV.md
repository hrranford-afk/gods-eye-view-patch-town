Why the Scholar's set leads: each attacker climbs a different way and each defence answers one of them, so the player has a real choice where to build. It also uses the real sofa (front edge, sides, behind the cushions).

## Not picked, and why
- "Lolly Stick Ladder" as a defence (the Dell Coder): it helps the attackers climb. Kept as a funny accident, not a defence.
- "Freeze" upgrade (the Dell Builder): fine, but the Double Sling uses the room better.
