# Round Table Kingdom: the modes (Harry's vision, 20 Nov 2026)

Three ways to play, one engine. AR is kept for being in a room with friends.

## 1. Friends match (same room, AR or table on screen): EQUAL
- Both sides get a base of the same value. Options: (a) "Make mine for me": the computer generates a fair base from a budget and a seed; (b) pick one of a few ready-made layouts; (c) build your own with the same budget.
- Fairness rule: every ready-made or generated base has the same build-point value and has been tested against the whole Arsenal by the bots (report card must pass: no weapon wins by more than X, no weapon is useless).
- New players: choose a layout, press Fight. Learn by watching.
- AR lives here: it is the "friends in the room" mode.

## 2. Home town (solo, offline): FARM AND BUILD
- A small city sim on the table: your mice make weapons and keep them firing. Workshop, Stockpile, gunners, menders (economy plan in DEFENCES_PLAN.md).
- Little games to make things (a few short mini-games: crank, sort, balance, aim). Mice level up in jobs.
- You design and save your bases here ("My bases"), and test them on the range.
- Your base defends itself while you are away; reports show what happened (text replay).

## 3. Raid (async, share-by-text first): BATTLE WHEN YOU WANT
- Raid a friend's base or a robot's (RAID_MODE_PLAN.md). Not real time.
- Step 1 (no server): bases travel as text you paste. Safe, free, private.
- Step 2 (only if Harry says yes): a small online service so bases can be found and raided without pasting. That needs a server = money and security, so it is Harry's call. Needs: no personal data, no chat, no names beyond a nickname, parental-style safety, nothing stored that identifies a player.

## Robot base designer (do first, Lab)
The bots design a few bases for each budget, test each against the Arsenal on the range, keep the fair ones as the ready-made layouts. This is the equal-base source for mode 1.

## Order
Arsenal and counters (running) -> Test my base and report card -> robot designs ready-made bases + "My bases" saves -> Home town economy (v0) -> Raid by text -> (Harry's call) online.
