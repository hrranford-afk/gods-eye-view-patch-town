### 2. The 3 Biggest Warnings
- **Watchtower Placement Risk:** Placing it too close to the table edge or without adjacent walls makes it an easy, early target (The Dell Quick Builder, The Dell Surveyor).
- **Unprotected Support Buildings:** Leaving the Workshop or Tent unguarded invites raids that halt repairs and healing, crippling your team’s sustainability (The Dell Reasoner, The Dell Quick Builder).
- **Risky Structure Collapse:** Structures like Bridges or Booby-Trapped Barricades can backfire by blocking your own mice or damaging your camp if hit (The Dell Builder, The Dell Quick Builder).

### 3. Proposal
Build and attack should interlock through the **Workshop-Watchtower Repair Loop** (The Dell Reasoner, The Dell Elder). Each round, the Workshop constantly patches the Watchtower’s supports, making it a high-value target that rewards sustained defense. Players choose launcher ammo dynamically: use **Bouncing Balls** (The Dell Quick Builder) to ricochet into supports, **Confetti Bombs** (The Dell Elder) to blind guards for a **Raiding Party** (The Dell Quick Builder), or **Sleep Dust** (The Dell Surveyor) to neutralize mend mice. Camp layout dictates tactics: place the **Gate** as a braced chokepoint (The Dell Surveyor) to funnel raids, hide a **Box House** (The Toymaker [a]) for secure storage, and risk a **Bridge** (The Dell Quick Builder) to access the central hill for flanking shots. The **Tent** (The Dell Quick Builder) serves as both a fast-healing zone and ambush cover. Victory comes not from a single shot, but from balancing repair pressure, ammo selection, and positional risk, ensuring every mouse has a clear, engaging role throughout the three rounds.
