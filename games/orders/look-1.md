## Context (the same for every model, under 170 words)
A new AR game for iPad, for two friends in one real lounge. Each player builds a small fort on their own sofa
from household odds and ends: cushion walls, matchbox towers, bottle-cap turrets, sock flags with no writing.
Then they take turns firing across the room at each other's fort. Each player picks a weapon kind: a catapult
that lobs over cushions, little rockets that fly fast and straight, or a swarm of tiny paper planes that fly
round things. Forts are built from what the players gathered first. It is toy war: things get knocked over,
squashed and splatted, nobody gets hurt and nobody dies. The look must be original, never a copy of any game.

## Stage 1 subjects
1. sofa fort: a tiny fort on a sofa seat, cushion walls, matchbox towers, a flag with no writing
2. weapons: catapult, rocket launcher and paper-plane swarm, side by side, each thumb-sized
3. the hit: a shot landing on a fort, soft splat, bits flying, funny not scary

## Stage 1 styles
A. Base Camp: an original 1990s TV cartoon, wobbly ink outlines, grubby painted textures, dust-bunny crew
B. Toy box: shiny bright plastic toys, chunky shapes, like a toy soldier set with no real army look
C. Paper craft: cardboard, paper, string and tape, folded and glued, visible cut edges
