# Brief for order mega-review-1 (under 6 KB, for the Town Brain)

## What we have (16 Nov 2026)
- **An AR room engine on the iPad (through the HelloXR app).** It scans real surfaces, remembers up to 8 rooms and finds its place again with a printed or on-screen sticker (this worked on Harry's iPad today) or with room patterns (this failed in his shed). It also spots everyday objects. Depth (things hiding behind the real sofa) arrives tonight.
- **Base Camp:**
  - a colony of tiny creatures living on the furniture: economy, market, seasons, battles (turn-based lobbing with toy weapons);
  - a Mac version with a 6-room virtual dolls' house and bots that play it.
- **Baguettes & Ballistics:** toy cars with guns, a kitchen race, two players with controllers.
- **Vigil:** a squad board game against an AI.
- **Ideas waiting:**
  - **Sofa Keep:** the player is the king on the real sofa. They build walls and turrets on the floor while waves climb up. A family version, plus a scary adult version.
  - **Dragon defence:** co-op.
  - **Raid** and **Hold the table.**
  - **Mega projects** built over a week.
- **One shared kit, many modes:** the room brain, bases, units, weapons, physics, controls and a test world.

## What is weak
- It has only been tested in pretend rooms. Real rooms (sheds, plain carpet, dim light) fail.
- Testing waits for Harry. Field reports do not yet reach the Mini automatically.
- There are no other players yet, and no reason to come back daily except the colony.
- The town's answers: about 1 in 9 seats come back empty, the slowest resident takes 48 minutes, the picture maker failed its canary 3 times, and many sparks are noise.

## The rules
- Family games: nobody dies, nothing burns.
- Our own creatures, places and weapons only: no copies, brands or real landmarks.
- People are counted, never tracked.
- Nothing personal goes into any model.
- Money, security, deleting and downloads are Harry's calls.
