# Order mega-1: mega projects and the builders' yard

From the game session, 15 Nov 2026. Read games/base-camp-MEGA_PROJECTS.md first (Harry's idea and the rules).

1. Each Dell resident: invent ONE week-long mega project the colony builds together from household things.
   Give it a name, what it is made of, 5 stages it goes through (what you see at each stage), and the moment
   when it is finished. Our own ideas only: no real landmarks, brands or famous buildings.
2. Each resident: 5 house parts the builders' yard sells (what it is made of, what it costs in buttons, planks,
   corn or rope, and one funny line the shopkeeper says about it).
3. The Town Brain: pick the 3 best projects and say why in two lines each.
Rules: nobody gets hurt, nothing burns, no loose batteries or flames. Words stay as written, each with its maker.
