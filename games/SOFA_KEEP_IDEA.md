# Sofa Keep: siege defence idea (Harry, 16 Nov 2026)

## Harry's words, kept
"Just imagine how good would that be if you could build your little base up around the floor and they try and get up on the sofa to try and get you. And you have your little turret on the top you're shooting, but you build your base. There's potentially two of you trying to hold, stop people getting in. I could join base build, or could just be one player at the moment."

## Games to learn from (mechanics only, nothing copied)
- Thronefall: build by day, defend by night; simple and calm.
- Toy Soldiers: tower defence on a toy diorama, where you can jump into a turret and aim it yourself.
- Orcs Must Die! and Dungeon Defenders: build traps and defences, then fight beside them; co-op.
- Kingdom Rush: towers along paths, upgrades, heroes.
- Rampart: rebuild the walls between waves.
- Stronghold: castles, gates, walls and sieges.
- Bad North: small, clear island defence where the shape of the land decides the fight.
- The feel of a famous fantasy wall siege (ladders, the breach, help arriving at dawn), in our own world with our own characters.

## How it fits the room
- The sofa is the keep. The floor and rug are the approach. The coffee table is an outpost.
- The engine already finds the heights (floor, seat, top of the back), and the climb points are the sofa's front edge.
- Attackers climb with lolly-stick ladders, string grapples and towers of themselves. Depth lets them sneak behind cushions.
- The player builds walls, gates and traps on the floor from kept resources. Toy traps: glue, bubbles, custard, rubber bands. Nothing burns.
- Turret on the cushion top: aim it yourself (iPad, mouse or pad), like the Base Camp slingshot or B&B guns.
- Nobody dies: bonked attackers tumble onto the rug and crawl home grumbling.
- Day: gather and build. Night: the waves come. Dawn: help arrives.
- Players: one now; a second player joins to build or to take a second turret (Mac + iPad with the screen anchor).
