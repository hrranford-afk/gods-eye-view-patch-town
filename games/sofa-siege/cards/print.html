Print the six cards.
1. Paper: A4, upright. Black and white is fine. Matte paper is best.
2. Scale: 100% ("Actual size"). Do not pick "Fit to page".
3. Cut each sheet across the middle. Each half is one card (210 × 148.5 mm).
4. Check with a ruler: a cut card should be 210 mm wide. If it is not, the scale was not 100%.
