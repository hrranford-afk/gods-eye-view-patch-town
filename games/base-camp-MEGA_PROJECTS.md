# Base Camp: mega projects and the builders' yard (Harry's idea, 15 Nov 2026)

Harry, in his words: "They just keep gathering them until they build stuff out of them... gather enough resources
and build like a little mega project together... You could take it back down. It'd give you some real meaning to what
they're doing over the week... everything's made piece by piece... a little shop that they buy parts for the house
and keep adding to it. Be like little workmen."

## The rules this sets
- Resources are kept, not lost. Spoiling and theft stay small; the store just grows until it is spent on building.
- A **mega project** is a week-long shared build, made piece by piece (about 100 to 200 pieces). Each piece is carried
  and fitted by a creature you can watch: scaffolding, a pencil crane, a rope hoist.
- When it is finished: a celebration that is kind to every tradition. Then it stays, or the player takes it down;
  taking it down gives most parts back and unlocks the next project.
- **The builders' yard**: a shop where creatures buy house parts (door, window, roof tiles, chimney, porch, a second
  floor) and add them to their own homes one at a time. Houses grow over days.
- Projects are our own, never copies of real landmarks: e.g. a lattice tower made of paperclips, a cotton-reel
  lighthouse, a matchbox bridge across the rug, a clock tower made from a cereal box.
- Works in AR (stays in your real room, kept by room snapshots) and in the dolls' house on the Mac.

## Order of work
After the Mac version and dolls' house. Uses the economy retune (resources kept, not drained).
Town order mega-1 asks the residents for projects, the pieces, and the house parts.
