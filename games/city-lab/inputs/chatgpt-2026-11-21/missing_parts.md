# City Lab — missing parts and unrepresented behaviour

These are **gaps**, not new recipe keys or new part kinds. All ten recipes use only the 22 published kinds and the 14 published materials. The proposed additions below **do not appear in the JSON**. They require separately reviewed builders and tests if chosen.

## Important schema-wide gaps

- A recipe simulates **one building**. It cannot link another building's output to its input, place roads, route vehicles or balance a shared city grid. Those are future city-level systems, not part kinds.
- Damage is intact-or-missing per part, not progressive per-brick physical fracture. These examples are **modular visual assemblies**, not a proof of rigid-body physics.
- Only `rotY` exists; it rotates around the up axis, so it cannot create a genuine upright rotating windmill sail or sweep a lighthouse beam.
- There are no occupants/citizens, demand for housing or education effects in the current schema. Do not infer them from the building name.
- The recipes have deliberately few parts (8–19), so they are small validation test cases rather than detailed finished artwork.
- Before adding any kind, follow `RECIPE.md`: whitelist/default size, reviewed builder, then validator and page tests; **never** put executable instructions into JSON.
