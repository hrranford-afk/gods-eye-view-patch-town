# Prompt for ChatGPT: "living island" world look, concept pack 6 (21 Nov 2026)

Written by the game session for Harry to paste into ChatGPT.

---

This is concept art for a new game on the same engine as Round Table Kingdom (the toy-mice tabletop game you made art for before). It is played on a real table seen through an iPad. The world is a small living toy island with a little town, on the table. Little mice live, work and build there. Weather rolls in. Buildings are wooden toys that come apart bit by bit. We have not chosen the exact game yet, so this pack fixes the LOOK of the world. Please read the style rules first:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hrranford-afk/gods-eye-view-patch-town/game/micro-precinct/games/ART_PLAN_ROUND_TABLE.md

**Same look as before:** hand-painted miniature toys, warm wood, soft shadows, light from the upper left, bold shapes that read when small.

**Rules:** nothing copied from any existing game (name, characters, art, music, levels, look). No real brands, logos or landmarks. No real people. Nobody gets hurt, no fire, no blood. No text or lettering inside the pictures (you cannot spell it reliably).

**Make 12 pictures, numbered 01 to 12, 1536 x 1024 PNG each:**
01 The island on a ROUND wooden table, seen from a three-quarter view, the island fitting the circle with a little spare wood around it.
02 The same island on a SQUARE table.
03 The same island on a long RECTANGULAR table, with spare space at both ends holding small things (a toy boat dock, a crate pile).
04 Town centre close-up: five small wooden houses of different shapes, a market stall row, a windmill, a harbour with two toy boats. Mice at work.
05 Mice close-up: six mice with different jobs (carrier, builder, gardener, sailor, baker, lookout), each with simple clothes and a tool. Show faces clearly.
06 Weather strip: the same corner of the island in four moods in one picture, calm, rain, storm, night with lanterns.
07 A cottage coming apart in stages, four panels left to right: whole, roof tiles loose, wall off, collapsed. Cheerful, not scary.
08 A tablet held over a real wooden table showing the island (an augmented reality view). Use a plain blank tablet frame, no logos.
09 A friend on each side of a table, two people seen from behind, each with a tablet, looking at the island from opposite sides.
10 Building kit sheet: 12 pieces to build with (wall, roof, door, window, crate, barrel, boat, jetty, lamp, flag, fence, tree), isolated on a plain cream background.
11 Icon sheet: 12 simple game icons (hammer, acorn, wheat, boat, sun, cloud, lightning, house, mouse, coin, gear, heart) on a dark brown background, bold outlines.
12 A mood board of the island at four times of day in a 2 x 2 grid.

**Please check your own work and put the results in the handover**
1. State for each picture which rules it meets: no text in the picture, nothing from an existing game, no real brands.
2. Make a contact sheet of all 12 small.
3. Pictures 10 and 11 must have clean edges on a plain background so pieces can be cut out. Say whether they do.
4. List the SHA-256 of each file in a manifest.

**Send back as one ZIP:** `pictures/` (01 to 12), `contact_sheet.png`, `manifest.json`, `HANDOVER.md` saying plainly what is art, what you checked and what is not tested. These are candidate looks only, until the game session has used them.
