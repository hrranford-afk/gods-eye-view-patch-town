## 4 Reading the table for island characters
- Rocky wooded: Stockholm archipelago or Penobscot Bay (conifers on granite, many islands, short hops). Differ on tide: near zero versus about 3 m.
- Low sandy: Wadden islands (tide 1.5 to 3.7 m, dunes, tidal flats) or the machair coast of the Outer Hebrides (tide larger, rain far higher, no trees).
- Farmland: Orkney (low green islands, strong tidal races) and Scilly (hedged small fields, mild).
- Exposed skerry: Shetland and the outer Hebrides: treeless, peat, heather, long sea fetch.
- Sheltered harbour: Scilly's main sound and Penobscot Bay harbours; Shetland voes.
Climate placing for the game's "temperate Atlantic look": Hebrides, Orkney and Shetland sit around 9.5 to 11.5 C annual mean maximum with 1,000 to 1,260 mm rain (VERIFIED for the three stations). Scilly is warmer and drier (PLAUSIBLE).

## 6 Suggested design ranges (my own summary, not a source)
Island width 100 m to 3 km, spacing 50 m to 5 km, tide 0 to 5 m spring (PLAUSIBLE span of the seven places), rain 500 to 1,300 mm a year, mean daily max 9 to 12 C for the Atlantic look. These are a union of the places above; each fictional island group should pick one column set so its numbers stay consistent.
