# Idea heritage: where the game's ideas came from

A running record so every idea can be traced and credited. Sources: OWNER (the owner's own words and playtests), TOWN (the local residents, from HW-14, signed notes in research/town_notes_hw14/), CHATGPT (its rounds: Castle Sound, the opening frames and camera plan, the sea and salvage brief), CAIRN (the cloud engineering resident), HARDWARE (Hardware Claude and its measurements). Where an idea has several parents, all are listed. This file records what is known from the project's own notes; where a source is unclear it says so.
