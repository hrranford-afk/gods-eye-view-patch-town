# The Gift: the vision (north star, v0, 24 Nov)

This is the page to steer by. It is meant to be read in five minutes. Everything else (design, build lists, research) serves this. Tags: OWNER = said by the owner; CAIRN = my reading.

## The one line
You are given a small island and no explanation. What you make of it is yours.

## The promise to the player
1. **A real place.** You can fly over it, walk its beach, watch the weather and the tide change, and the sea and light look the way a quiet cold coast looks.
2. **A real gift.** Nothing was earned. That is the feeling: doubt, then trust, then responsibility.
3. **A real choice.** Holiday island, sanctuary, homestead, workshop of repaired old things, or (later) a dug-in refuge. None is the right answer.
4. **A real clock.** Help fades over about four years. You find out what you and the island can become.

## The arc (what a whole game feels like) CAIRN
1. **Before:** an ordinary day, a plain envelope, a test that works, one bag packed, a boat. About 8 minutes. The mainland shrinks.
2. **Arrival:** dawn on a beach. A crate and a brass key. A letter in plain words. The ledger: cash, and delivery credit that can only buy deliveries by boat.
3. **First day:** get dry, find water, look around, repair one plank, order a thing, fix an old machine, build one thing, first night under a faint lighthouse light.
4. **Years one to four:** seasons, storms, finds washing up, neighbours on the chart, strangers after a storm (rarely). The catalogue thins. Local making takes over.
5. **After:** a year-end report with four lenses (profit, wildlife, happiness, resilience). No single winning ending. The world may go wrong, slowly work out, or land in between, and it all should show on your island.

## The helper (the hidden heart) OWNER + CAIRN
- A hidden system that looks after the world and expects trouble. It is never shown, never has a face, never speaks, and the game never answers what it is.
- Its fingerprints: a small plain mark on crates, letters and old robots; deliveries; bottle messages slightly too well informed; one parcel that arrives before it was ordered.
- The question underneath is: is help a gift or a leash? The player decides. Mild trouble only: late parcels, thin seasons, a dark lighthouse.
- Robots are relics with no faces and no feelings. They are the helper's old hands, never explained.

## How it looks and feels (north star) CAIRN
- A round tabletop island in a cool sea, seen from a raised three-quarter view, like high-end photography of a hand-made model. You can lean in to ground level.
- **The sea is the star.** Real waves, foam, shore wash, boats riding the water. This is where the Radeon VII earns its keep.
- Light is the second star: dawn, gold hour, fog, rain, rainbow, a clear cold night, the lighthouse beam.
- Clutter with meaning: weathered timber, moss, rust, tidelines, finds. After a storm the shore is messy, then you tidy it.
- Sound (not built yet): sea, gulls, rope, a fog horn, quiet.
- Hopeful, a little mysterious, never frightening. Nothing gory or cruel.

## Where you play it
- AR on a real table (lightest).
- iPad and phone (touch first).
- A computer with a good graphics card.
- A Radeon VII (and a 4K lounge TV) for the full showcase.
One game, one save. Different graphics tiers only.

## The world beyond your island OWNER
- Scroll the planet as a sea chart of parcels (island groups). Survey voyages reveal parcels. Land can be bought, leased or found. Price comes from how good the land is.
- Zoom in and the tabletop opens. What washes up depends on where you are on the planet.

## Hard truths (so we stay honest) CAIRN
1. It is a very big game. The first day is not the game; it is the first proof of feeling.
2. The middle (years two and three) needs its own reason to keep going. Not solved yet.
3. The mystery can be spoiled by explaining it. Keep it unexplained.
4. We cannot measure real speed from the cloud. The Radeon reports are our truth.
5. Writing and feeling come first. Features serve them.

## What exists today vs what is needed
Exists: tabletop worlds, weather, sky clock, lighthouse, boats, repair jetty, camp mode with robot yard, seals (not yet in the page), ultra tier and device presets, Radeon benchmark, aftermath debris stress test (in progress), parcel chart prototype, opening-hour script, story sources.
Being built now: the first day (ledger, delivery, yard, one build, first night).
Needed: real waves, seals in the page, better houses, the prologue, saving across years, the chart view, sound.

## Roadmap (stages, not dates) CAIRN
1. **Proof of feeling:** the first day plays well on iPad and Radeon.
2. **Water and light:** real waves, richer sky, aftermath.
3. **Year one:** seasons, one vision (holiday or sanctuary) end to end, the report card.
4. **The chart:** buy land, survey voyages.
5. **The years after:** thinning help, the middle, the endings.
6. **Later modes:** dig-in refuge, co-op raiders, quiet repair-ruins.

## The cover brief in one breath
A quiet invitation: a beach at dawn, a crate and a key, a faint light on a headland, a sea that looks real. Hopeful, warm, a little mysterious. No faces, no helper shown, title "The Gift".
