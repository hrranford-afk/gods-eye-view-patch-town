# The Gift: the core loop plan (owner focus, 24 Nov)

Owner said: stick to the core bits. Building. A boat to zip around in. The whirlpool so storms bring stuff. The little robots: a short mission unlocks them, then they collect the right things for building.
One job at a time. Each job ends with something the owner can press and play. Aims are guesses.

## Job 1: First day plus robot orders (running)
Done when: the owner can arrive, read the letter, fix the jetty plank, order a delivery, fit the missing part so a robot starts, give it a collection order (planks, rope, glass, tins), see stock counts rise, build one thing that spends that stock, and sleep through the first night. Saves and loads.

## Job 2: A boat you can zip around in
Done when: the owner can take the small tender from the jetty and drive it round the islands (thumb stick on iPad, keys on desktop), with a believable wake and bobbing on the waves, landing at a beach, carrying a small load (robots' stock or a crate), and a small engine part that makes it faster. Wildlife and weather react. Uses boat_motion.js and the boat kit. Starts as a rowing boat or small found engine; the pole-drive skiff and the tuning shop come later.

## Job 3: Storm and the haul (the whirlpool)
Done when: a storm builds (weather mood), a tidal swirl and surge sweep in, a haul of debris is left along the wrack line (reuse the aftermath debris work: planks, barrels, crates, rope, nets, one hulk later), and the robots can be told to gather it. A storm is foreshadowed by weather. Wreckage and cargo only, nothing gory.

## After that (not now)
Playthrough pass to find gaps; real waves; pole-drive skiff and tuning shop; the Hoard depot island; chart and buying land; activity islands.

## Rules for every job
Default page unchanged (hash 8d962c0c, 88,790 triangles). Push in small safe steps. Honest pictures with flaws. Robots are relics: no faces, no speech, no feelings. People counted not tracked. Hopeful tone.
