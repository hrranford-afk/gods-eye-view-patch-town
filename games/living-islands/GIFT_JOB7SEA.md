# Job 7: the sea into the Gift, step 1 (26 Nov 2026). One line per pushed item.
1. SHARED WAVE CODE: sea_waves.js (pure, no THREE: tier tables iPad 8 / desktop 16 / Radeon 24 / MAX 48 waves, the eight weather inputs, buildWaves, disp, height with the pinch inverted by an 8 step fixed point, normal, orbit, retune and advance so a moving sea never jumps phase). Test: tests/sea-waves-test.js (24, pure node: matches a straight reference sum and a separate solver within 1 mm, deterministic).
2. THE SEA IN THE GIFT (iPad tier first, all tiers wired): islands_look.html, switch `&waves=1` on, `&waves=0` off, ON by default only with `game=gift` (sea_waves.js loads only then); the Gift sea is the same fragment shader (sky, sun, fog, shallows, foam, weather patch) behind a polar wave mesh (vertex Gerstner sum, waves flatten toward the shore and fade with distance, troughs darker, foam from folded crests and wind streaks); the old flat disc stays for `&waves=0` and the default page. Weather: the page's weather state (windMs, name) and the storm layer set the wave inputs (calm days stay calm, showers and rain a squall, storm a gale, the big storm the Great Wash waves, fog glassy) and ease toward them over about 5 s with the wave phases kept. The shoreline foam band also climbs and falls with the swell. `__li.sea` exposes on, tier, h(x,z), n(x,z), hs, tris. Numbers (software GL page, scene triangles from the page's own counter, device=ipad Gift): 70,853 with `&waves=0`, 93,765 with the wave sea (the sea is 23,040 triangles, one mesh, one draw call, replacing the 128 triangle disc: draw calls unchanged); default page unchanged: hash 8d962c0c, 88,790 triangles. 
3. BOAT, CRATES AND WRACK RIDE THE WAVES: boat_drive.js (rock(b,t,amp,caps,surf): with the wave sea the hull heaves by exactly the sea height and pitches and rolls with the slope, caps .25 to .55 rad instead of .06 to .14, the old swell and caps are unchanged without a wave sea), physical.js (floating crates and floating timber and rope, and the tender that leaves with the crates, ride the height and tilt of the water), gift_day.js (the delivery tender rides it), islands_look.html (the shoreline foam band climbs the beach with the swell, in item 2). Test: tests/sea-ride-test.js (12, pure: heave within 0 cm of the height at 200 places and headings in calm, squall and gale, limit 5 cm; a gale lists the boat more than twice as far as a calm day; pitch is bow up on a rising slope); boat-drive-test 97, storm-test, physical-test 92 unchanged (no test expected flat water, none edited). NOT done: washed-up timber lying on the sand does not move with the swash (only floating pieces ride); moored scenery boats and the kit boats of the page's own harbour keep their old bobbing.
4. RADEON AND DESKTOP TIERS: islands_look.html (tier from `&device=`: ipad 8 waves and a 72 x 160 mesh, desktop 16 waves and 140 x 320, radeon 24 waves and 220 x 480 with 10 pixel ripples' worth of chop bands; spray points on the GPU that live on folded crests, iPad 600, desktop 4,000, Radeon 12,000; the foam settings are the test page's (whitecaps need a folded crest AND height AND noise, thin trailing foam, sparse wind streaks, darker troughs); `__li.sea.force(name, s)` for tests). Numbers on a software renderer (counts, not speed): iPad Gift 93,765 scene triangles (70,853 with `&waves=0`; under the 150k budget, so the old sea did not need trimming), sea 23,040 triangles and one draw call; desktop sea 89,600 triangles, scene 202,601, rendered 256,655 triangles in 324 calls (ultra path, shadows); Radeon sea 211,200 triangles, scene 349,089, rendered 423,053 triangles in 281 to 308 calls; the draw-call count of the iPad page was not measured (the sea replaces one mesh with one mesh). Smoke by hand at 834x1062 touch (iPad), 1280x720 desktop and radeon: zero console errors at calm and in a storm on all three. A boat on the sea, live page, phases frozen: within 3.3 cm of the height query over 12 samples (with live phases on a 1 fps software renderer it drifts up to 14 cm because the boat and the sea are updated in different frames; at 60 fps that is under 2 cm).
WHAT IS WRONG (honest, from looking at the pictures; they are in /tmp only, shots/ not updated): (a) the opening bay is a tidal flat at dawn, and the waves are flattened toward the shore on purpose, so the arrival view shows a brown shallow sheet with a lace of foam and no visible swell in either weather: I could not get a frame that shows open swell, so the look of the waves is unverified by eye; (b) the storm frame has long horizontal streaks across the far water and the far shore (could be wind-streak foam, could be a screenshot taken mid frame; not settled); (c) the water reads brown-red at dawn, never teal, but the storm water colour is not yet slate; (d) spray points were not seen in any picture; (e) moire grain and the faint surf foam of the prototype were not checked at the Gift's angles; (f) the CPU height ignores camera-distance fade of waves longer than 6 m, fine near the boat; (g) real device speed is unknown: the pixel shader is the old sea's plus a few terms, the vertex stage is new.
