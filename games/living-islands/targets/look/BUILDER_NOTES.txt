THE GIFT
Visual targets. Prices, costs, counts, telemetry and times are illustrative. Touch targets need testing in the live UI.

01_Dawn
Art and implementation — Boats, camp and water have prototype references. The pearl dawn light is a finish target.
Required UI — Show the first floating plank when steering begins.
Clarity pass — Keep the finished deck clear of spare boards so the shortage reads.

01b_Bright
Art and implementation — The sea geometry carries the scene. The bright caustics are a lighting target.
Required UI — Show the pickup affordance within rowing range.
Clarity pass — Release the painter from the rock so the rowboat reads as free.

01c_Drizzle
Art and implementation — Use the existing coast and weather system. Fine droplet highlights are illustrative.
Required UI — Keep the same input and pickup hint across weather changes.
Clarity pass — Give the floating board one light edge against the grey water.

02_Ferrying
Art and implementation — Boat and yard are established assets. Loaded draft and the cargo procession are targets.
Required UI — Show cargo aboard and confirm each unload.
Clarity pass — Let the moving crates trace the route and retire the arrows after first use.

03_Dock
Art and implementation — Jetty repairs have a prototype. String tension, joinery and lantern detail are finish targets.
Required UI — Advance the work stage only when stock arrives.
Clarity pass — Keep the same shore landmark visible at every stage.

04_Night
Art and implementation — The scene builds on the night sky and lighthouse tests. The bottle glint is a flourish.
Required UI — Offer a pickup only within reach of the bottle.
Clarity pass — Keep the lamp the brightest point.

05_Haul
Art and implementation — The storm and shore have visual references. The full salvage strand and hulk are proposed placements.
Required UI — Identify a selected find and its carrying limit.
Clarity pass — Leave clear walking space between the high-tide line and the hulk.

06_Mainland
Art and implementation — These five mainland sets are proposed. Wet paving and sodium light link them.
Required UI — Allow advance, pause and skip through the prelude.
Clarity pass — Repeat the carpet pattern once at the office as a quiet visual link.

07_First_day
Art and implementation — Boat and sea come from the gameplay references. The gold ring and polished dock are visual targets.
Required UI — Day, menu, stock, one hint, steering and contextual Dock.
Clarity pass — Dim Dock while out of range and clear timber from beside the stalled carts.

08_Catalogue
Art and implementation — Paper, lamps and illustrated goods are interface art. The two balances follow the game rules.
Required UI — Select a good, inspect price and arrival time, then order once.
Clarity pass — State which balance will be charged before confirming.

09_Letter
Art and implementation — The island can sit behind a static paper layer. Paper grain and the brown stamp are art direction.
Required UI — Readable letter and a large Close target.
Clarity pass — Keep the short passage together at the chosen text size.

10_Build
Art and implementation — The five thumbnails are concepts for structures. Timber framing on the panel is decorative.
Required UI — Each row needs cost, purpose, required stock and stage; only one active build.
Clarity pass — Replace repeated material headings with a single column heading.

11_Town
Art and implementation — Island terrain has a prototype. Settlement density and the warm roof treatment are illustrative.
Required UI — Show both balances, happiness, counted population, five goods and the arriving boat.
Clarity pass — Use the approved title and fold secondary controls into Menu.

12_Desk
Art and implementation — Chart, camp and ledger are proposed as simultaneous views. Desk lighting is staged.
Required UI — Keep the route at left, play at centre, ledger, frame times and events at right.
Clarity pass — Label live versus sample readings and let the player enlarge the graph.
