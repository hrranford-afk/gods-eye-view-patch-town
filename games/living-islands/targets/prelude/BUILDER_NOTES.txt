THE GIFT — PRELUDE
Concept targets. The mainland sets and interactions are proposed.

Night workbench
Art and build — Lamp bloom and wet reflections are finish targets. The bench can use static meshes and one hand animation.
Interaction — Tap the loose belt to inspect its fit.
Clarity or kindness — Leave room around the belt for a finger.

Morning workbench
Art and build — Use the same camera and meshes with cooler window light. Steam is optional.
Interaction — Resume the same repair at its saved step.
Clarity or kindness — Keep the work area bright enough to see the screw heads.

Doormat
Art and build — Use a parcel mesh and stamp decal. Wet fibres are close-up polish.
Interaction — Tap the parcel to unfold its flap.
Clarity or kindness — Let the spare part show before asking the player to pick it up.

Neighbour
Art and build — Sleeves and hands can carry the handover. Fine wool is illustrative.
Interaction — Accept the hoover, then set it on the bench.
Clarity or kindness — Leave the tea on the ledge so the exchange feels unhurried.

Inheritance
Art and build — Paper, key and tape can be layered sprites. Rain shine is illustrative.
Interaction — Lift the key, then unfold either paper.
Clarity or kindness — Keep the office and personal letters available to reread.

Stamp close-up
Art and build — One small decal can repeat on all three objects. Macro depth of field is optional.
Interaction — Tap to inspect each impression.
Clarity or kindness — Match size and gap across materials without prompting a conclusion.

One bag
Art and build — Four item meshes and a bag layer can carry this scene. Cloth creases are polish.
Interaction — Choose a keepsake, then close the bag.
Clarity or kindness — Allow the player to leave the last pocket empty.

Street
Art and build — Repeat the runner motif on the awning. Wet amber reflections are art direction.
Interaction — Pause by the café or return the wave.
Clarity or kindness — Hold the doorway in view long enough for a wave back.

Station
Art and build — Bench, holdall and kiosk can be a single set. Fine rain is a mood layer.
Interaction — Pick up the bag when ready to board.
Clarity or kindness — Leave time for one last look towards the kiosk.

Quay
Art and build — The prototype supplies a boat and sea vocabulary. Lantern reflections are a lighting target.
Interaction — Tap the waiting boat to board.
Clarity or kindness — Let teal enter at the waterline before filling the frame.

Crossing
Art and build — Island, boat and camp reference the game. Fine spray and dawn grading are polish.
Interaction — Steer towards the missing deck section.
Clarity or kindness — Keep the robot silhouette clear against the sand.

Six stamps
Art and build — All six can be flat single-colour decals. Worn edges are optional.
Interaction — Use a zoomed inspection view when discovered.
Clarity or kindness — Pick the broken oval. Its gap survives reduction and suggests an imperfect tool.

Stationery
Art and build — Typeset paper layers can be shown directly. Stock colour distinguishes the two voices.
Interaction — Tap either letter to enlarge or close it.
Clarity or kindness — Keep the handwriting generous and offer plain-text reading.
