# Bake bridge pilot: result (23 Nov 2026)

Pilot of `research/BAKE_BRIDGE.md` section 6 on island seed 1122, the hero camera, sun az 320 el 20. Blender 5.3 (CPU Cycles, a loaded 4-core box), three.js r128, headless Chromium with software GL. Nothing was run on a GPU, an iPad or Blender 3.6.

## Short answer
GO for making the split bridge the live look of the land, with caveats below. The land matches Cycles far better than today's look on every number the note set, including the half-of-today's-EMD rule, at one island of 3.9 MB. The things that are NOT solved are the sea and sky (out of scope, still far from Blender), trees and boulders (flat colours, not baked), and everything that needs a real device.

## What worked
* **Split bake in linear, tone curve in the page.** Light map (sky GI + AO, sun off, no Color) + albedo + sun mask + normal. The land reads as a Cycles picture: soft AO under trees and boulders, blue-grey shaded slopes, sun shadows in the right place with the right length, no clipping on the sand. C versus the Blender ground has median dE 2.3.
* **Sun moves, bake does not.** D (sun 0) and E (el 35, light map unchanged, mask channel G, SH and light-map tint from the el 35 panorama) both track their own Cycles renders (dE 2.0 and 1.3, EMD about .01). The sun mask blends by elevation (triangular, 15 deg spacing), fades over 25 deg of azimuth away from the baked 320, and hands over to the live shadow map there (`mix(shadowMap, 1, amount)` so the two never multiply: failure mode (e) did not appear). I did not test sun azimuths other than 320 or elevations between the baked ones.
* **White card (derivation of the PI scaling): confirmed.** Cycles: a white diffuse card under the sky renders (0.460, 0.621, 0.695), the DIFFUSE bake (Direct+Indirect, no Color) of it gives the identical (0.460, 0.621, 0.695), under the sun alone (12 W/m2, colour 1 .76 .5, el 20) it renders 1.306 x colour, exactly S sin(el)/pi. Page (`bridge_whitecard.html`, no tone map, linear output): the light map texel (0.462, 0.624, 0.694 after 8-bit sRGB coding) shows as (0.463, 0.627, 0.690): r128's `lightMap` times PI times Lambert 1/PI is exactly albedo x texel, no scale needed; a DirectionalLight of intensity S/PI shows 1.325, 1.012, 0.667 against Cycles 1.306, 0.993, 0.653 (1.5 percent, the 8-bit read-back). So: lightMapIntensity 1, sun intensity = S/pi, with `physicallyCorrectLights` left false. Test: `tests/bridge-test.js`.
* **Tone curve.** A 65-entry table (0.25 EV steps, -10 to +6 EV) fitted from a wedge pushed through the scene's Filmic "Medium High Contrast" with `Image.save_render` (16 bit), replaces the body of the ACES chunk so every material (and a no-post path) gets it; table values are linear display values because three applies the sRGB encode afterwards. Max error on the grey row 0.0008 (sRGB units); on sand, grass, sky-blue and the sun colour at all exposures at most 0.008, mean about 0.001; r128's ACES at 0.9 on the same wedge is off by 0.028 on grey and 0.03 on those colours (mid grey 0.18 shows 0.469 against Filmic 0.498). Only pure saturated primaries (R, G or B alone, 0 on the other channels, not a case the land has) are off (mean 0.03, max 0.6), and a luminance-dependent desaturation term did not help enough to ship (not used). Exposure is 2^-0.4, the hero's.
* **uv2.** Blender 5.3 exports the second UV set as `TEXCOORD_1` when two layers exist (verified in the GLB JSON; r128 GLTFLoader gives `uv2`, asserted by the test, no JS copy needed). Note both layers are the same planar atlas (with the 16 px gutter inset), so for this island it proves the plumbing, not a different second layout.
* **Sizes.** GLB 1.77 MB (land + trees + boulders, albedo JPEG q90 inside; the old GLB was 1.80 MB), light map JPEG q92 4:4:4 0.35 MB, normal JPEG q80 0.78 MB, sun mask PNG 0.94 MB, panorama preview 21 KB, depth map 32 KB, `bridge.json` 2.4 KB: 3.88 MB for the island.
  Light map: PNG 2.36 MB, JPEG q92 4:4:4 0.353 MB (4:2:0 0.286 MB) against the 7.4 MB PNG of the old bake, a saving of 95 percent (of the PNG of the same light map 85 percent). JPEG error in linear: mean 0.004, max 0.045, no banding seen in the shaded areas (failure mode (b) did not appear). The normal map is a bad PNG candidate (8.6 MB, bump noise at 3.6 cm texels), fine as JPEG.
* **Renderer cost** (`renderer.info`, software GL): 5 draw calls, 47,566 triangles, 6 textures (3 in A), 4 programs. Triangles equal today's baked page. GPU memory by arithmetic: albedo, light map, normal, mask at 2048 RGBA with mips is 4 x 22 MB = about 90 MB for the island (the T0 row of the tier table; T1 would drop the mask, T2 would use 1024).
* **Tide.** The bake has no sea, so the seabed is lit as dry ground with sky GI, AO and the same shadows: ground the tide exposes is correctly lit. Band areas (`assets/bridge/tide_band.json`): below -1 m 2,152 m2 (mean light .565, sun visible .96); -1 to 0 m 203 m2 (.530, .95); 0 to +1 m 230 m2 (.503, .84); above +1 m 551 m2 (.391, .97 at el 20). What is NOT handled: wetness (a wet band should be darker and glossier, live uniform on the albedo, not built), the seabed seen THROUGH the water is lit as dry sand by the sky and sun (the sea shader mixes it in at its alpha; it looks fine in the shots, but real shallow water would be darker and caustic-lit), and the sea's own shadows. Also the sun mask has no information about the sea level, so nothing changes with the tide: that is the point, and it holds.

## What failed or is weak
* **The note's failure modes**: (a) not seen (light map and uv2 honoured); (b) not seen; (c) seams: checked only at the hero camera, not at every zoom or mip; (d) not seen on land colours; (e) avoided by the `mix`; (f) not testable here.
* **Trees and boulders are flat colours** (hand-darkened in JS by 0.62, 0.8 and 0.5, TUNING) with live sun, the SH probe and a shadow map, no AO: they look like toys next to the Cycles ones (leaf noise, moss, cracks, dark undersides). This is the biggest visible land-adjacent gap now.
* **Sun mask softness.** The 1 degree Cycles sun gives hard edges; I blurred the mask by 2 px (sigma) at pack time to approach the Cycles penumbra; edges are still a little crisper than Cycles.
* **Ground detail.** The normal map carries the bump of the Cycles material but at normalScale 0.45 (TUNING) the page is grainier and lacks the crack lines of the reference (visible at 1:1).
* **Mean luminance within 0.03 is only just met (0.027)**: the page ground is slightly lighter (the sand at the sun edge). Not tuned further.
* **Light map noise.** 64 samples left about 4 percent grain; a 1.2 px gaussian at pack time removed it. A real denoise would be better.
* **Shadow bake samples.** The first shadow bake at 64 samples took 34 minutes per sun position on the loaded box and returned a mean of 1.0013 for a visibility pass (above 1: sample-count dependent normalisation?). I killed it and used 16 samples (9 to 11 minutes each, mean .95, .91, .94 for el 20, 35, 50); I did not investigate the difference. Bake times here are not representative: the box ran at a load of 8 to 13; the old bake did 48 samples in 411 s on four free cores. Light bake 2,492 s, albedo 142 s, normal 127 s, panoramas 3 s each, wedge instant.
* **Page grade, not the whole picture.** The sky strip, far sea and near sea are far from Blender (see numbers). A is not "the live page": it is today's lighting recipe on the SAME mesh and albedo (the real live page has its own terrain and vertex colours); I did this to isolate lighting. The default island of `islands_look.html` was not rendered.
* **Not tested**: rotation shimmer (the baked light is view independent by construction; not looked at), other sun azimuths, Blender 3.6 (patterns are 3.6 compatible, `S()` helper not needed in the new scripts, nothing 4.x only called except a try/except for the panorama type; unverified there), LI_DEVICE/LI_GPU_NAME (honoured through `reset()`, CPU used), iPad decode and memory, fps (software GL only: every frame in this run took seconds, which says nothing).

## Limits
Software GL (SwiftShader) only; the page loaded in about 2 seconds locally with all files on localhost, so there is no network or decode timing. No device. One island, one camera, one azimuth. Reference renders are 64 to 96 samples with denoise. The committed shots are the Blender image palettised where needed to stay under 1.3 MB (`bridge-blender.png` is 256 colours; the metrics in `bridge-metrics.json` were computed on the unpalettised renders, so rerunning the compare on the committed shots moves numbers by a little).

## Recommendation
GO for the land: ship the split bake (albedo + sky light map + sun mask + normal, sun, weather, tide, sea live), the fitted Filmic chunk, SH9 for movers, as the live look of T0 and T1, and the same files at 1024 for T2. Keep `baked_land` beauty (unlit) for T3 to T5 (it is the cheap look, and B is 11.6 dE from Blender). Do not claim the whole picture matches: sea, sky and trees do not yet.

3. Weather and the sky clock: the pilot has the hooks (`uSkyTint`, `uMaskAmt`, SH interpolation by sun elevation); next, drive them from `SKY_CLOCK.md` (sun position and colour), bake SH9 sets for overcast and dusk, feed `uMaskAmt` from the weather (0 in rain), multiply cloud shadow into the sun term, and add the wetness uniform for tide and rain.
