Macro sea: seven days of weather from outside the window

A pure function of (seed, game hour). Storms, swell, tide, surge, whitecaps and arrivals all come from macro_sea.js. 1 game hour = 20 real seconds in the game; here the cursor moves faster so you can watch.

Storm timeline

Top strip: storm centres passing (S), warm (W) and cold (C) fronts, rain. Swell lines run in order of period, longest first, so a distant storm shows as a long, low swell that rises, peaks and shortens before the wind comes. Dotted vertical lines are day starts. Everything is analytic: no stored state.

Sea slice at the cursor

The water surface from seaH (the same function the GLSL snippet mirrors) across a 1 km window at the cursor hour. Vertical scale is exaggerated (the factor is printed in the corner, it adapts so calm and storm both fit); wavelengths are the diorama map (the honest ones are in the state). The island shadow is not modelled here: no refraction, no lee, no breaking on shoals.

Five sea states on one coast

Constructed scenarios (no seed storms): calm and fair come from quiet-weather hours of the seed; rough, storm and storm with swell arriving come from one slow low passing 300 km north, at three of its hours.
