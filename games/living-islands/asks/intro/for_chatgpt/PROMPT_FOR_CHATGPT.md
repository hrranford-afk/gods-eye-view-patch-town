# More help with the opening of The Gift (ideas round)

Thank you for the eight frames, the camera plan, the colour list and the sea-and-salvage brief. They were wonderful and we are building from them. One story change: the whirlpool belongs ONLY to the legendary Great Storm (calm frames have a normal sea), please re-render frames 01, 02, 03 and 08 without it, keep a weak fading swirl in 06 and 07, and keep 04 and 05.

Now we would like your IDEAS for the opening (the 90 second film that leads into play). Our first cut works as a timed film but looks like blocks; we are building worn details (robots, hulk, jetty, stock bays, camp) in code. Please give us:

1. TURNAROUND SHEETS: front, side, back and three-quarter views, plus a close-up of wear details, for (a) the hauler robot, (b) the sorter robot, (c) the stacker robot, (d) the stranded hulk, (e) the jetty with piles and rope wraps, (f) the four stock bays. Original, worn, warm; robots old and plain with ONE lamp or lens eye, simple tracks and a jointed gripper arm, NO mouths, no speech grilles, no human faces.
2. TEXTURE SHEETS we can use as references: tileable rusted metal, salt-worn wood, mossy rock, wet sand, rope, cloth, painted ochre with chips (256 or 512 px, plus a short note on what makes each one look unique rather than repeated).
3. DETAILS WE HAVE NOT THOUGHT OF: for each of the eight shots, five small things that would make a viewer look twice, without any text or speech.
4. THE TITLE MOMENT: how the game title could appear (no text on objects; the title can be a quiet card) and the first two seconds after the player takes control.
5. SOUND AND MUSIC NOTES for each shot (what is heard, what is not), no speech.
6. WHAT TO CUT: anything in the plan that is too much for 90 seconds.

Rules: hopeful, cosy, awe plus a little danger; nothing gory; nobody hurt on screen; no hidden helper shown; no real brands or places; original designs only. Please do not include any personal information.

Send back one zip: the sheets (JPEG, about 1920 px), the ideas as plain text, and a short README.
