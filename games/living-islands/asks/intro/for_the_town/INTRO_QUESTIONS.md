# Questions about the opening (for the town; can be added to the current round or run on its own)

The game opens with a 90 second film: night at the lighthouse, dawn over four islands, the colony working at evening, a long swell, the Great Storm (the legendary one, with a whirlpool that belongs to it alone) breaking the jetty, the wreck at grey dawn, arrival by boat, and the first repair (a battery fitted into a robot's lamp-eyed head). Read context/ for the plan, the camera plan and how the first cut looked.

Answer what moves you. Be original: say what nobody has said yet, in your own voice, signed.

1. What should the player feel in the first ten seconds, and what single sound or image would give that feeling?
2. What small, quiet thing happens in the colony shot that tells us these machines and people are cared for, without a word of speech?
3. How would the islanders tell the moment the Great Storm came? What did they see first, hear first, do?
4. What is in the wreck at grey dawn that rewards a second look (small details, traces, a story told without text)?
5. What is the very last second of the film, before the player takes control?
6. If the opening were a piece of music made of the island's own rhythms, how would it begin, build, break in the storm and return?
7. What should the opening NOT do?
