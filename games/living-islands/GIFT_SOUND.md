# The Gift: sound (job "wire sound into the gift")

Written without being able to listen. Software GL and a fake audio module only: nobody has heard any of this. Every number is a first guess for the owner's ears; "What to listen for" is at the end.

Files: `gift_sound.js` (pure core plus the page layer), a probe in `gift_day.js` (`soundProbe`, the one place that knows where the game keeps what the sound needs), the existing `audio.js` and `audio_song.js` (not edited). Tests: `tests/gift-sound-test.js` (node, fake audio, a scripted day) and `tests/gift-sound-page-test.js` (page).

Switch: `game=gift` loads `audio.js`, `audio_song.js` and `gift_sound.js`; `&sound=0` keeps them out; `&sound=1` loads them on any page (only the chip and the audio unlock; the beds and events need the gift's state). The default page (no switches) loads no audio: hash 8d962c0c and 88,790 triangles are unchanged.

## What was done, one line each
1. Unlock and settings: a "Sound on" chip at the foot of the top-right column (hidden once the sound runs, and while the Menu is open); the first touch, click or key anywhere also starts it (audio.js listens in capture); a Sound panel in the Menu (Master, Effects, Music, a mute toggle); settings in `localStorage` under `li.gift.sound` (try/catch); the context is suspended while the tab is hidden; with no AudioContext the chip never shows and nothing throws.
2. Ambient beds: `soundProbe` reads the wave sea's weather name and how far it has risen (`__li.sea.name()` and `.s()`, the inputs sea_waves.js reads), the weather's wind speed, rain from the weather state, night from the sky clock's sun elevation, and the storm layer's quiet level. The core turns them into `setSeaEnergy`, `setWind`, `setRain`, `setNight` four times a second (audio.js ramps them, so there is no zipper). In the arrival bay the sea bed is kept gentle (0.10 + 0.35 of the full value) and only a storm opens it to the full sea. The lighthouse beam hum is audio.js's night bed; each lamp sweep (every 12 s while it is night) sends a long note to the song (`LI_SONG.lighthouse()`). The gift has no turning beam, so the sweep is heard, not seen.
3. Events, each fired from a real change in game state and throttled (a table in `gift_sound.js`, at most n per s): crate thud when the stock total rises (3 a second at most); plank fit when a build stage completes or a jetty plank is fitted; the winch two-note 0.55 s after the plank for the stages that lift (the frame, the finish, and a build that leaves the site); the hold loop in one-second segments while a finger holds (a hand pick, the fit at the post, or any physical-layer press); a UI tick on a tap of any button or select in the HUD (8 a second at most); the delivery horn when the ship comes in (the old separate fog-horn context in gift_day.js is no longer made when the sound layer is on), the boat creak when the tender lies alongside and when you board the rowing boat, a wake every 3 s while you row; the brass key when the lid is lifted and when it turns in the hut door; the rock hum (pitch follows how far the storm has built, 0 to 1) before a storm, the roar (level = storm) and thunder every 14 to 34 s once the storm is strong; gulls are audio.js's own rare gulls (every 8 to 25 s by day, fewer in wind). Robots: see "Robot kinds" below. Nothing is built per call (one option object is reused) and a refused event (voice cap) is just dropped.
4. The island song: `setIsland(seed)` once, `setTide` from the page's tide, `setStorm` from the storm layer's quiet level, `setNight`, `robotBeat` when a working robot starts, changes kind or state or its battery moves 0.05, `lighthouse()` at each sweep, `cue('winch'|'key'|'thunder'|'delivery')` with the matching events, and the week beat from the game day. Music defaults to 0.35 and the song is stopped (not just silenced) while muted.

## Robot kinds (how the page's robots map to the audio's three kinds)
The page's robots are carriers with a role. Role `sorter` is the audio's `sorter` (soft rattle, tick, thunk). A robot lent to the physical layer that is placing a unit (`twork`) is a `stacker` (almost silent load shifting). Every other working robot (walking out, carrying, dropping, at work on a hauler role, or walking a unit for the physical layer) is a `hauler`. Idle, at the charging bay, stalled at the dock, or switched off: silent. State: battery under 22 percent is `tired`, rain is `wet`, else `work`. At most 4 robots sound at once (audio.js caps its robot voices at 6). The scrape under a moving robot follows the ground: planks on the dock, sand low on the beach, pebbles above that, grass higher still.

## Week beats (the calendar has no weekday names)
The game calendar has twelve 30-day months and no weekdays, so the sound cuts the day count into weeks of seven: days 1 to 3 of a week are tender's day (the song's opening phrase), days 4 and 5 low-tide days (the quiet middle), days 6 and 7 lighthouse days (the variation). Day 1 is tender's day.

## audio.js additive change (asked for by the opening film)
`LI_AUDIO.ambient(on)` (default true) turns audio.js's own random gulls and crickets off and on, and `LI_AUDIO.setSeed(n)` makes its randomness (those, robot jitter, noise buffers made afterwards) repeatable. Nothing else changed; with the defaults the behaviour is as before. The only edit to audio.js in this job.
