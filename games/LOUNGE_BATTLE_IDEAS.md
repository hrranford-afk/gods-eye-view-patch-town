## How it could fit
- Each player builds a base on their own sofa (a seat surface the room scan already finds).
- Bases are built from what was gathered, as in Base Camp's economy (corn, wood, crafting).
- Turn-based lobbing, as in Base Camp's battle mode, so two iPads only send small messages.
- Weapon families are a choice for the player: catapult, rockets, or swarms of little planes.
- Our own creatures and weapons; never a copy of an existing game.
