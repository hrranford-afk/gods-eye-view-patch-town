# Vigil

A turn-based squad game in 3D, set in a painted miniature city on a hobby desk. Six Wardens cross a ruined city to a
gunship. The Brood's hive mind learns where you like to stop, and lays traps there. It has fog of war, an armoury,
drones, and dice odds shown before you commit.

- **Source:** Harry's claude.ai artifact "Vigil" (https://claude.ai/artifact/CtW3wHmFJ3REGooBVHBTDX), version
  1793628393-a96b, last updated 2 Nov 2026. It was copied here on 12 Nov 2026 as a backup, at Harry's request ("Save").
- **Play:** open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js r147 from jsDelivr, and Google Fonts.
- **Saves:** the page keeps the hive's memory in the browser's local storage, under `vigil.v2` and `vigil.v2.game`.
- **Credits:** the ideas from the PatchTown sparks are credited in the game, under "Ideas from PatchTown".
- **Ideas for later:** an AR mode, so the city stands on your real table; a box in the arcade.
