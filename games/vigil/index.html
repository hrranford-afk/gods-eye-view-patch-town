    Mission · The Drowned Quarter

    VIGIL

    Six Wardens are cut off in a ruined city. The gunship can only land on the far side. Between you and it, the Brood is waking, and its hive mind learns how you move.

    GoalReach the landing pad in the far corner and extract as many Wardens as you can. The swarm grows every round. TurnEach Warden has 2 actions: move, and attack once. Pick a soldier, then a square or a creature. You see the odds before you commit. FogYou only see what your soldiers can see. The Scout sees furthest. WeaponsArm each Warden before the battle: flamers, plasma, sniper rifles, grenade launchers, a thunder hammer and more. MedicHeals the most wounded soldier next to her. DronesTake two thinking drones: Mason builds cover, Sentry guards with a turret, Lark heals and stretchers the fallen to safety. EnemiesSwarmlings, Spitters and Brutes, plus Leapers that jump walls, Bloaters that burst in acid, Burrowers that strike from underground, and the Brood Mother. ListenThe Hive hums your own moves back at you, and its hum rises as it learns you. Something may be waiting where it expects you to stop. SporesSpitters lob spores at the squares where the Hive predicts your soldiers will stand. Be hard to predict.

    Ideas from PatchTown

    These came from the PatchTown sparks: nine tiny expert models, each trained on different books. Their exact words:

    The ListenerA hidden creature that waits where it expects you to stop, strikes once, then vanishes. "It would lay itself at his feet at the point where the wall had been broken." The Naturalist. "It would listen as though it was waiting for a call to arms." The Musician. Living diceEvery exact guess gives the swarm an extra die. "Whenever a creature lands a turn, a die comes to life." The Choreographer. The marchThe Hive hums your squad's own moves back at you, closer each time. "This learned sound gradually developed into a tune, and the tune, with a minor third added, gradually became the 'Soldier's March.'" The Synthesist. "On the third night, it would sing the same song." The Sonero. The humThe Hive's hum rises as it learns you and cracks when it guesses well. "Each turn the swarm hummed a little louder, until the very pitch of the note was lost." The Musician. Sound in the fogYou hear creatures move in the dark as ripples. "In the fog, the little drone could only see the small, wailing cello." The Composer.
