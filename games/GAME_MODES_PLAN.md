"I think we should have a few to play with, maybe a few different styles of play. It wouldn't be great if we could like shoot some dragons or things, or we could have bases and we've got to run over to their base and shoot each other. I mean, there's loads of different concepts we could mess around with, isn't there? But they're all the mechanics are built on the same thing, aren't they? Just different game styles."

## The shared kit (built once, used by every mode)
1. Room brain: surfaces, spots, clutter, room memory, sticker / screen / title-screen lock, depth.
2. Bases: built piece by piece from kept resources (Base Camp economy, builders' yard).
3. Units: our own creatures and toy vehicles, with simple brains.
4. Weapons: lob, bounce, flick, swarm, tape swing (Base Camp battle), guns and rockets (B&B).
5. Physics: gravity, wind, bounces, real clutter as cover.
6. Players: touch, mouse and keys, controllers, split screen, Mac + iPad sharing one room through the screen anchor.
7. Rules: nobody dies (dizzy, pop home), toy weapons, our own characters.
8. Test world: dolls' house, bots, nightly reports.
